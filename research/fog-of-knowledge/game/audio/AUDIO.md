# Game audio assets

## rain-light.ogg / rain-storm.ogg
Source: "soft rain drops.wav" by bruno.auzet on Freesound
https://freesound.org/people/bruno.auzet/sounds/664162/
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Recording notes (author): very light rain; foreground drops on wood/metal,
gentle gutter flow further back. Stereo AB (2x EM172 on Zoom F3), 48 kHz / 24-bit,
France Nov 2022.

Game processing: trimmed mid sections, loudness-normalized (light softer than
storm), mild high-shelf cut to avoid bathroom-hiss, crossfaded for seamless loop.
Encoded as Ogg Vorbis. No YouTube rips.

Why this sample: Reddit/ASMR listeners prefer distinct soft droplet hits
("pluq pluq") and canopy/ground texture over constant white-noise shower hiss.
This field recording matches that; procedural highpass noise does not.
