# Fiber, crafting and climbing

Wildflower stems are common, persistent, gatherable fiber resources across grass and forest, independent of graphics settings. A separate appended random stream preserves prior resource indices. Hover the flower and press E; distant pickup walks to it. A collected stem stays removed when the chunk reloads.

Backpack crafting has a Climbing Craft Path filter and expandable process descriptions. Inputs and heat/tool requirements are checked before consumption. Assembly cards explain the completed process; F inspects 3D gear. Hands do not need to be pre-equipped for crafting. Keeping climbing gloves in inventory equips the pair and unlocks the hand-over-hand steep-terrain pose and resistance to terrain sliding. Without gloves, steep uphill input is blocked. The first steep encounter opens a minimizable guide, reopenable from Backpack.

The chain covers bark extraction, fiber thread, hide tanning, stone hammer, copper/tin/bronze/iron processing, tongs, needles, spikes, stitched leather gloves and reinforced climbing gloves. Copper/bronze/iron knives use existing durability mechanics and substitute for stone knives. Existing axe tiers remain available. Heat checks display both Celsius and Fahrenheit.

## Scientific scope

This is a simplified gameplay model, not a real crafting or climbing manual. Times for tanning, mold preparation, metalworking and sewing are compressed. Iron bloom reduction and consolidation are distinguished from melting iron. Generic game inventory units do not specify actual alloy percentages. The climbing attachment is a game rule and is not a safety guarantee for spiked gloves in real life. The terrain is a heightfield; this implements steep ground climbing rather than arbitrary overhang traversal.
