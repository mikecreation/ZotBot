Noh
(musical theater, music (performing arts genre), ... Associated Concepts (hierarchy name))
Note:
Important form of classical Japanese dance drama that has been performed since the 14th century. It is often based on stories from traditional literature with a supernatural being transformed into human form as a hero telling a story. Noh integrates masks, costumes and various props into a dance-based performance, Emotions are mainly conveyed through stylized conventional gestures.

..... Belangrijke vorm van klassiek Japans dansdrama dat sinds de 14e eeuw wordt opgevoerd.
