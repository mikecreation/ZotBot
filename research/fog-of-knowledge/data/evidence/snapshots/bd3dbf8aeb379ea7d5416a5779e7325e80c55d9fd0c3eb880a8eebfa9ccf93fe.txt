GBD-tree
GBD-tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A generalized
BD-tree
, hence the name, which stores spatially extended objects as a hierarchy  of
minimum bounding boxes
.  It is a balanced multiway tree which serves as a
spatial access method
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
spatial access method
.

After
[GG98, page 211]
.
