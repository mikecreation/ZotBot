planarization
planarization
(definition)
Definition:
The process of transforming a
graph
into a
planar graph
. More formally, the transformation involves either removing edges (planarization by edge removal), or replacing pairs of nonincident edges by 4-starts (planarization by adding crossing
vertices
).  In both cases, the aim of planarization is minimize the number of edge removals or replacements.
