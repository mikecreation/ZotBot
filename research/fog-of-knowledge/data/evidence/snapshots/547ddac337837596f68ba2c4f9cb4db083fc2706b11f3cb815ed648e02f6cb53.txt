shadow heap
(data structure)
Definition:
A
heap
, implemented in an
array
, adjacent to an unordered table.  The
shadow
is the table
nodes
and all their (recursive)
parents
, by array index, in the heap.
See also
shadow merge insert
,
shadow merge
.
