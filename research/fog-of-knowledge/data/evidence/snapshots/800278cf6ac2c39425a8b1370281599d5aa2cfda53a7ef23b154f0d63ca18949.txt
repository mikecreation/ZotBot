ideal random shuffle
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
permutation
algorithm, or shuffle, that has exactly the same chance of producing any permutation.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
randomized algorithm
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
Fisher-Yates shuffle
.
Note:
Attaching random tags then sorting (see
permutation
) may not work: if tags may be duplicated, a deterministic sort will not randomly switch the order of elements with duplicate tags.

Historical Note
Formerly called "perfect shuffle".  Renamed in January 2009 when Dave Bayer pointed out that the term is almost universally used to mean dividing a list of elements (or deck of cards) exactly in half then precisely interleaving the two halves.  This has been the use of "perfect shuffle" for decades.
