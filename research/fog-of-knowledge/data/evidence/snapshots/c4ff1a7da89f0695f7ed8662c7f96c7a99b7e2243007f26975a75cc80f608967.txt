queue
queue
(data structure)
Definition:
A collection of items in which only the earliest added item may be accessed.  Basic operations are add (to the
tail
) or enqueue and delete (from the
head
) or dequeue.  Delete returns the item removed.  Also known as "first-in, first-out" or FIFO.
Formal Definition:
It is convenient to define delete or dequeue in terms of remove and a new operation, front.   The operations new(), add(v, Q), front(Q), and remove(Q) may be defined with
axiomatic semantics
as follows.
new() returns a queue
front(add(v, new())) = v
remove(add(v, new())) = new()
front(add(v, add(w, Q))) = front(add(w, Q))
remove(add(v, add(w, Q))) = add(v, remove(add(w, Q)))
where Q is a queue and v and w are values.
Also known as
FIFO.
