k-connected graph
k-connected graph
(definition)
Definition:
A
connected graph
such that deleting any k-1
vertices
(and incident
edges
) results in a graph that is still connected.
See also
biconnected graph
,
triconnected graph
,
cut vertex
.
Note:
Informally, there are at least k independent
paths
from any vertex to any other vertex.
