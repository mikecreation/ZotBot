directed acyclic graph
(definition)
Definition:
A
directed graph
with no
path
that starts and ends at the same
vertex
.
Also known as
DAG, acyclic directed graph, oriented acyclic graph.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
directed graph
,
acyclic graph
.

cycle
,
feedback edge set
,
feedback vertex set
.
