partial order
partial order
(definition)
Definition:
An order defined for some, but not necessarily all, pairs of items.  For instance, the
sets
{a, b} and {a, c, d} are
subsets
of {a, b, c, d}, but neither is a subset of the other.  So  "subset of" is a partial order on sets.
Formal Definition:
A partial order is a
binary relation
that is
reflexive
,
transitive
, and
antisymmetric
.
See also
total order
,
poset
.
