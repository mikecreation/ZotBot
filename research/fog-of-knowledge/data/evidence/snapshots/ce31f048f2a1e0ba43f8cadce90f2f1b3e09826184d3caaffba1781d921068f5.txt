Scope Note
Hematologic diseases and diseases of the lymphatic system collectively. Hemic diseases include disorders involving the formed elements (e.g.,
ERYTHROCYTE AGGREGATION, INTRAVASCULAR
) and chemical components (e.g.,
BLOOD PROTEIN DISORDERS
); lymphatic diseases include disorders relating to lymph, lymph nodes, and lymphocytes.
