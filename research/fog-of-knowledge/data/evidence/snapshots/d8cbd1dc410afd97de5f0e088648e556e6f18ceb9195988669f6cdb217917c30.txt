Viterbi algorithm
Viterbi algorithm
(algorithm)
Definition:
An algorithm to compute the optimal (most likely) state sequence in a
hidden Markov model
given a sequence of observed outputs.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
hidden Markov model
.
See also
Baum Welch algorithm
.
Note:
Also used to decode, i.e. remove noise from, linear error-correcting codes.
