strongly connected graph
(definition)
Definition:
A
directed graph
that has a
path
from each
vertex
to every other vertex.
Formal Definition:
A
directed graph
D=(V, E) such that for all pairs of
vertices
u, v ∈ V, there is a
path
from u to v and from v to u.
See also
connected graph
,
strongly connected component
,
bridge
.
