in-place sort
in-place sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
sort
algorithm in which the sorted items occupy the same storage as the original ones.  These algorithms may use
o(n)
additional memory for bookkeeping, but at most a constant number of items are kept in auxiliary memory at any time.
Also known as
sort in place.
