Scope Note
Conditions characterized by disturbances of usual sleep patterns or behaviors.
SLEEP WAKE DISORDERS
may be divided into three major categories:
DYSSOMNIAS
(i.e. disorders characterized by insomnia or hypersomnia),
PARASOMNIAS
(abnormal sleep behaviors), and
SLEEP WAKE DISORDERS
secondary to medical or psychiatric disorders. (From Thorpy, Sleep Disorders Medicine, 1994, p187)
