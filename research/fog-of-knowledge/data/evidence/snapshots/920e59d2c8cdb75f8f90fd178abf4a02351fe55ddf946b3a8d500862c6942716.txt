Sri Lankan
(South Asian, Asian, ... Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Culture and style of an Indo-Aryan ethnic group native to the island country of Sri Lanka. They possess a 2,500-year written history characterized by grand ancient kingdoms, pioneering hydraulic engineering, and a foundational role in the preservation of Theravada Buddhism.
