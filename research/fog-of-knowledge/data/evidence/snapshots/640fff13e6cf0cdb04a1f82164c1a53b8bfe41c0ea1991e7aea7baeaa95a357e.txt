religious ideologies
(religious concepts, <religions and religious concepts>, Associated Concepts (hierarchy name))
Note:
Organized bodies of ideas and attitudes having to do with religion, which is broadly defined as belief systems that encompass various personal and institutional relationships between human beings and what they regard as holy, sacred, or divine, usually but not always a deity, or a spiritual or occult force.
