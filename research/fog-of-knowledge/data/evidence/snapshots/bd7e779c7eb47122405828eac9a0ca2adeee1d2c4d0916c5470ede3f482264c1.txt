direct chaining
direct chaining
(data structure)
Definition:
A
collision resolution scheme
in which the
hash table
is an
array
of
links
to
lists
. Each list holds all the items with the same hash value.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
separate chaining
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
hash table
.
See also
linear search
.
Note:
The items in the list may be searched and maintained with any list search algorithms. Any searchable data structure may be used instead of a list.
