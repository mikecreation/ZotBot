order-preserving Huffman coding
(algorithm)
Definition:
A variable-length character coding based on the frequency of each character.  The algorithm is similar to
Huffman coding
, but the trees are kept in the same order as the characters.  Two
adjacent
trees with the least combined frequency are joined as subtrees of a new root.  As with Huffman coding, that new tree is assigned the sum of the subtrees' frequencies.  Repeat until all characters are in one tree.

(... is a part of or used in me.)
full binary tree
,
priority queue
,
greedy algorithm
.
See also
Huffman coding
.
Note:
This may not produce a true Huffman code.  Although encoded messages may be up to twice as long as true Huffman codes, order-preserving Huffman codes are "quite effective for most data."
[Graef06, page 5]
There are algorithms to produce optimum order-preserving prefix codes, but they are more complicated.
