Markov chain
Markov chain
(data structure)
Definition:
A
finite state machine
with probabilities for each transition, that is, a probability that the next state is s
j
given that the current state is s
i
.
See also
hidden Markov model
.
Note:
Equivalently, a
weighted, directed graph
in which the weights correspond to the probability of that transition.  In other words, the weights are nonnegative and the total weight of outgoing
edges
is positive.  If the weights are normalized, the total weight, including
self-loops
, is 1. After
[Leda98]
.
Author:
PEB
More information
Named after
Andrei Andreyevich Markov
(1856 - 1922), who studied poetry and other texts as stochastic sequences of characters.
