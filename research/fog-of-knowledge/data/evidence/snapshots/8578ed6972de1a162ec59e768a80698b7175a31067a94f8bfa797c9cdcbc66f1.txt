group identity
(identity, metaphysical concepts, ... Associated Concepts (hierarchy name))
Note:
An individual person's conscious sharing of some kind of essential character with one or more groups of other people, such as a race, nation, social class, profession, or body of people organized into a group at some particular time for some particular purpose.
