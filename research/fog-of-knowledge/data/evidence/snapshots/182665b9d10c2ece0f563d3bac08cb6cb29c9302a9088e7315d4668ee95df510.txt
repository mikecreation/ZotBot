Prim-Jarnik algorithm
Prim-Jarnik algorithm
(algorithm)
Definition:
Compute a
minimum spanning tree
by beginning with any
vertex
as the current
tree
.  At each step add a least
edge
between any
vertex
not in the tree and any vertex in the tree.  Continue until all vertices have been added.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
greedy algorithm
.
See also
Kruskal's algorithm
.

JLG
