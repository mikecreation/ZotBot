(a,b)-tree
(a,b)-tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
search tree
with the restrictions that all
leaves
are at the same
depth
and all
internal nodes
have between a and b
children
, where a and b are integers such that  2 ≤ a ≤ (b+1)/2.   The
root
may have as few as 2 children.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
search tree
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
B-tree
with 1 ≤ a.

Algorithms and Theory of Computation Handbook
,  page 4-18, CRC Press LLC.
