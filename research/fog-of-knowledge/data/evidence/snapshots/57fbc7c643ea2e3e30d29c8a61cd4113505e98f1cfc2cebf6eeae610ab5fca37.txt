text
text
(definition)
Definition:
A list of characters, usually thought of as a list of words separated by spaces.
See also
string matching
,
string matching with errors
,
longest common subsequence
,
inverted index
.
Note:
The term
string
usually refers to a small sequence of characters, such as a name or a sentence.  The term text usually refers to a large sequence of characters, such as an article or a book.
See definition relating to string matching in Algorithms and Theory of Computation Handbook, page 11-26.
