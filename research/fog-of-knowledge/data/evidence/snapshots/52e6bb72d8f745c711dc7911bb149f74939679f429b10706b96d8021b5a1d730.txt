lexicographical order
lexicographical order
(definition)
Definition:
Alphabetical or "dictionary" order.
Note:
For instance, ordering cities by name rather than, say, by country or population (attributes of the named entity).  The exact order, for example A-Za-z vs. aA-zZ etc., depends on the locale and application.
