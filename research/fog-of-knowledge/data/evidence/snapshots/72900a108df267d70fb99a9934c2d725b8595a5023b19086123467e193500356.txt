gates of hell
(doctrinal concepts, religious concepts, ... Associated Concepts (hierarchy name))
Note:
Entrances to the underworld as known in any of various religions and belief systems. The gates of hell may be either apprehended in a material sense to correspond to caves or other places on earth, or conceived of in only an abstract or spiritual sense.
