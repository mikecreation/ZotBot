order-preserving minimal perfect hashing
order-preserving minimal perfect hashing
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
minimal perfect hashing
function for keys in S such that if k1, k2 ∈ S and k1 > k2, then f(k1) > f(k2).
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
minimal perfect hashing
,
linear hash
,
Las Vegas algorithm
.
See also
Pearson's hash
.
Note:
For example, if the keys are stored in order in an
array
, the array offsets are an order preserving minimal perfect hash of the keys.
Author:
BJ
