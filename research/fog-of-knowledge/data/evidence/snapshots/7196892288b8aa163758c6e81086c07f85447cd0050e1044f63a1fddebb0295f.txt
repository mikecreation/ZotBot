restricted universe sort
(definition)
Definition:
A
sort
algorithm that operates on the basis that the
keys
are members of a restricted set of values.  They may not require comparisons of keys to perform the sorting.
See also
comparison sort
,
counting sort
,
bucket sort
,
radix sort
,
distribution sort
,
universe
.
