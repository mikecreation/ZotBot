proper
proper
(definition)
Definition:
(1) A subunit of a unit is not equal to the unit itself.  For instance, a proper
substring
is not the whole
string
, a proper
subset
is not the whole
set
, a proper
subgraph
is not the whole
graph
, etc.  (2) According to a rule, as in
proper coloring
.
