Archaeological Style
(<styles, periods, and cultures by general era>, Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Style of visual arts that intentionally imitates ancient objects or style. Most often used to refer to jewelry design, but may also be applied to paintings, sculpture, and occasionally to architecture.
