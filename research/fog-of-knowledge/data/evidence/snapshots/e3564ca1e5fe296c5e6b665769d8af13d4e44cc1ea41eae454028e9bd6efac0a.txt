omega
ω
(definition)
Definition:
A theoretical measure of the execution of an
algorithm
, usually the time or memory needed, given the problem size n, which is usually the number of items.  Informally, saying some equation f(n) = ω (g(n)) means g(n) becomes insignificant relative to f(n) as n goes to infinity.
Formal Definition:
f(n) = ω (g(n)) means that for any positive constant c,  there exists a constant k, such that  0 ≤ cg(n) < f(n) for all  n ≥ k. The value of k must not depend on n, but may depend on c.
See also
Ω(n)
,
little-o notation
,
big-O notation
.
Note:
This is the Greek letter Omega.
