Horner's rule
(algorithm)
Definition:
A polynomial A(x) = a

+ a
1

2
x² + a
3
x³ + … may be written as A(x) = a
0
+ x(a
1
+ x(a
2
+ x(a
3
+ …))).
Note:
A polynomial may be evaluated at a point x', that is A(x') computed, in
Θ(n)
time using Horner's rule.  That is, repeated multiplications and additions, rather than the naive methods of raising x to powers, multiplying by the coefficient, and accumulating.
