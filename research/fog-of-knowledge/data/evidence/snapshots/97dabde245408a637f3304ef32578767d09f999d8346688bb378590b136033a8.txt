symmetric set difference
(definition)
Definition:
Members which are in either
set
, but not in both.  That is, for sets A and B, it is (A - B) ∪ (B - A).
See also
difference
.
Note:
{2, 3, 5} Δ {7, 5} = {2, 3, 7}.
