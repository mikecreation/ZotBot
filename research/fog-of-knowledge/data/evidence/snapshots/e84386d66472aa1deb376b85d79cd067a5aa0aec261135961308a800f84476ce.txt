rho-approximation algorithm
ρ-approximation algorithm
(algorithmic technique)
Definition:
An
approximation algorithm
guaranteed to find a solution at most (or at least, as appropriate) ρ times the optimum. The ratio ρ is the performance ratio or
relative performance guarantee
of the algorithm.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
approximation algorithm
.
See also
Monte Carlo algorithm
.
