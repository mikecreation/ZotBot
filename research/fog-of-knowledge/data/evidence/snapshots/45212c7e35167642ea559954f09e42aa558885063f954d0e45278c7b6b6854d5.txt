recursive data structure
(definition)
Definition:
A
data structure
that is partially composed of smaller or simpler instances of the same data structure.  For instance, a
tree
is composed of smaller trees (
subtrees
) and
leaf
nodes
, and a
list
may have other lists as elements.
See also
iteration
,
recursion
,
recursive
.
