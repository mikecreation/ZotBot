metaheuristic
(algorithmic technique)
Definition:
(1) A high-level algorithmic framework or approach that can be specialized to solve
optimization problems
.  (2) A high-level strategy that guides other
heuristics
in a search for
feasible solutions
.
See also
optimization problem
,
simulated annealing
.
