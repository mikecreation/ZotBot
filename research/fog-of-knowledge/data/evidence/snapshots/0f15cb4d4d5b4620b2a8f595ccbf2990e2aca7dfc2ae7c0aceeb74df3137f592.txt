signature
signature
(definition)
Definition:
The types or
domains
, and order, in some representations, of inputs to and outputs from a
function
.
Note:
For instance, the signature of the C library function
strncmp()
(compare up to n characters of two
strings
) is
int (char *, char *, int)
.  That is, it takes two C strings and an integer, and returns an integer.
