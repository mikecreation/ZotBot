build-heap
(algorithm)
Definition:
Convert an
array
into a
heap
by executing
heapify
progressively closer to the
root
.  For an array of n nodes, this takes
O(n)
time under the comparison model.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
heapsort
,
JSort
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
heapify
.

After
[CLR90, page 145]
.
