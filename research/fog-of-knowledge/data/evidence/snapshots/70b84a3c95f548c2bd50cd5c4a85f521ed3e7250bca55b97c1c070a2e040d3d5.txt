list
list
(data structure)
Definition:
A collection of items accessible one after another beginning at the
head
and ending at the
tail
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
bag
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
linked list
,
doubly linked list
,
circular list
,
self-organizing list
,
ordered linked list
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
orthogonal lists
.
Note:
A list may have additional access methods.  A list may be ordered.  A list can be seen as an ordered
bag
.  A list may be kept as the leading items of an
array
, but inserting items other than at the end takes time.
