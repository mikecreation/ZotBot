jump list
jump list
(data structure)
Definition:
A variant of
doubly linked list
with items in sorted order and having two levels of additional links that span geometrically increasing distances.  For a  list with n items, the next level is a link from item i,  1 ≤ i ≤ n - ⌊ n
1/3
⌋, to  item i + ⌊ i
1/3
⌋.  At the top level, items 1³, 2³, 3³, …,  ⌊ n
1/3
⌋ ³ have backward links, that is, there is a link from item i³,  1 < i ≤ ⌊ n
1/3
⌋ ³, to  item (i-1)³.   Search, insert, and delete are
O(n
1/3
)
worst case.
See also
skip list
,
dictionary
.
Note:
The data structure can also be seen as a
binary search tree
with additional links.
