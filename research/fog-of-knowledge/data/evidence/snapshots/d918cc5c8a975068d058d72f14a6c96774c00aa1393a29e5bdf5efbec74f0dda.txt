efficiency
efficiency
(definition)
Definition:
The resources an
algorithm
used to find an answer.  It is usually measured in terms of the theoretical computations, such as comparisons or data moves, the memory used, the number of messages passed, the number of disk accesses, etc.

big-O notation
,
best case
,
worst case
,
average case
.
