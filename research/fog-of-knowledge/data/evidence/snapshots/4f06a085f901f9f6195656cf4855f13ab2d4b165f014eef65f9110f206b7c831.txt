biconnected graph
biconnected graph
(definition)
Definition:
A
connected graph
that is not broken into disconnected pieces by deleting any single
vertex
(and incident
edges
).
See also
cut vertex
,
biconnected component
,
triconnected graph
,
k-connected graph
.
Note:
Informally, there are at least two independent
paths
from any vertex to any other vertex.
