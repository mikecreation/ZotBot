Scope Note
Disorders caused by cellular or humoral immune responses primarily directed towards nervous system autoantigens. The immune response may be directed towards specific tissue components (e.g., myelin) and may be limited to the central nervous system (e.g.,
MULTIPLE SCLEROSIS
) or the peripheral nervous system (e.g.,
GUILLAIN-BARRE SYNDROME
).
