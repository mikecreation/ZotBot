introspective sort
introspective sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A variant of
quicksort
which switches to
heapsort
for pathological inputs, that is, when execution time is becoming quadratic.
Also known as
introsort.
Author:
PEB
More information
David R. Musser
,
Introspective Sorting and Selection Algorithms
, Software-Practice and Experience, 8:983-993, 1997.
