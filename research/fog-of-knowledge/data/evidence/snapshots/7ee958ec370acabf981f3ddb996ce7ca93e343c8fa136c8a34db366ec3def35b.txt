balance
balance
(definition)
Definition:
The (weight) balance of a tree is the number of
leaves
of the left subtree of a tree, denoted |T
l
|, divided by the total number of leaves of the tree.  Formally, ρ(T)  = |T
l
|/|T|.
Also known as
root balance.
See also
BB(α) tree
,
height-balanced tree
,
right rotation
,
left rotation
,
relaxed balance
.
Note:
The balance of a node is the balance of the (sub)tree rooted at that node. After
