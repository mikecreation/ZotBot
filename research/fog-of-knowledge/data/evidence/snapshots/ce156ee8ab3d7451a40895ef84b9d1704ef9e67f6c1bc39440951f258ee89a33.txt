permutation
(algorithm)
Definition:
A rearrangement of elements, where none are lost, added, or changed.  The
Fisher-Yates shuffle
randomly permutes elements.
Also known as
shuffle.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
combination
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
ideal random shuffle
,
Fisher-Yates shuffle
,
Johnson-Trotter
,
sort
,
derangement
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
American flag sort
.
See also
pseudo-random number generator
.
Note:
A
sort
is a permutation where the items are arranged in some order. A
derangement
is a permutation where no item is in its original position.
There are
n!
permutations of n (distinguishable) elements.
