Floyd-Warshall algorithm
(algorithm)
Definition:
An algorithm to solve the
all pairs shortest path
problem in a
weighted, directed graph
by multiplying an
adjacency-matrix representation
of the graph multiple times.  The edges may have negative weights, but no negative weight
cycles
. The time complexity is
Θ (V³)
.
See also
Johnson's algorithm
.
