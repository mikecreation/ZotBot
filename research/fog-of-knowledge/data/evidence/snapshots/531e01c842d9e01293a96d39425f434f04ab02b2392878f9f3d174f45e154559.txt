degree
degree
(definition)
Definition:
(1) Of a
vertex
, the number of
edges
connected to it.  (2) Of a
graph
, the maximum degree of any vertex. (3) Of a
tree
node
, the number of
child
nodes it has.
See also
in-degree
,
out-degree
.
