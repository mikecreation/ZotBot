difference
difference
(definition)
Definition:
The difference of
set
A minus set B is a set having all the members which are in A, but not in B.
See also
complement
,
union
,
intersection
,
symmetric set difference
.
Note:
For example, {2, 3, 5} - {7, 5} = {2, 3}.
