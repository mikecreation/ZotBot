elastic-bucket trie
(data structure)
Definition:
A variant of a
bucket trie
in which each
leaf
node for n strings is a
bucket
allocated to hold exactly n strings.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
bucket trie
.
Note:
The name comes from re
TRIE
val and is pronounced, "tree".  See the historical note at
trie
.
