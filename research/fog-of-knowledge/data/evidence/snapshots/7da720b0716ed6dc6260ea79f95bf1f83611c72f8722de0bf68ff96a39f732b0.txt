discrete space
The

The discrete topology is the
http://
planetmath
.org/node/3290
finest topology one can give to a set. Any set with the discrete topology is

⁢
(
x
,
y
)
=
1

,

∈

≠

(
x

x

The following conditions are

is a discrete space.
2.
Every

.
3.
Every subset of
X

is a

of
x
.
Note that any

Suppose
