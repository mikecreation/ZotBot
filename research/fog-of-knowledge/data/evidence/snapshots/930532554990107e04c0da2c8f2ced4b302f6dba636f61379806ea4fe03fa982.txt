Scope Note
A formal process of examination of patient care or research proposals for conformity with ethical standards. The review is usually conducted by an organized clinical or research ethics committee (
CLINICAL ETHICS COMMITTEES
or
RESEARCH ETHICS COMMITTEES
), sometimes by a subset of such a committee, an ad hoc group, or an individual ethicist (
ETHICISTS
).
