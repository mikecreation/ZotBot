BANG file
(data structure)
Definition:
A balanced and nested grid (BANG) file is a
point access method
that divides space into a nonperiodic grid.  Each spatial dimension is divided by a
linear hash
.  Cells may intersect, and points may be distributed between them.
See also
twin grid file
.
Note:
After
[GG98]
.
