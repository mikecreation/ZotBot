twisted tabulation hashing
(algorithm)
Definition:
Similar to
tabulation hashing

Note:
The twister value and the intermediate hash value can be accumulated in the same integer, since the combining operation is xor.
The tables have additional twister values, compared to tables used in
tabulation hashing

It doesn't matter whether it is the first or the last character that is treated specially.
