MODIFIND
MODIFIND
(algorithm)
Definition:
Select the k
th
smallest element of an array and
partition
the array around it.  Partition around the value of the k
th
element.  If the partition boundary is not at k, repeat in the partition that includes k.
See also
select and partition
,
Find
,
Select
.
Note:
This does fewer swaps than
Find
.
