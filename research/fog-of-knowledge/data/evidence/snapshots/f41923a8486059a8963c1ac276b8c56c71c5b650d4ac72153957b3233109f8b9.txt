pile
pile
(data structure)
Definition:
An ordered
deque
, that is, items may only be added  to or removed from the
head
or the
tail
. An item is added to the
head
if it is smaller than the current head.  An item is added to the
tail
if it is greater than the current tail.  Items are never inserted into the  middle, rather, an additional pile may be created.
See also
linked list
,
UnShuffle sort
.
