tournament
tournament
(algorithm)
Definition:
Find the maximum of a set of n elements in  ⌈log n⌉ "rounds" (passes) by "playing" (comparing) pairs of elements and advancing the winner (greater) of each pair to the next round.  It takes n-1 comparisons, like
linear search
, but may be parallelized, extended to also find the second greatest element, etc.
See also
select k
th
element
,
select and partition
.
Note:
The second greatest element can be found with log n - 1 additional comparisons.
