CTL
CTL
(definition)
Definition:
A propositional, branching-time temporal logic for which formulas can be checked in
linear
time.  An acronym for Computation  Tree Logic.
Note:
CTL differs from CTL* in that in CTL path quantifiers (E and A) always appear with temporal operators (F, G, X, U, etc.).  Therefore CTL formulas are
state
formulas.
