graph drawing
(classic problem)
Definition:
The problem of representing a
graph
in a plane "neatly," for instance with a minimum number of
edge crossings
.
See also
grid drawing
,
orthogonal drawing
,
straight-line drawing
,
planar graph
,
planarization
.
