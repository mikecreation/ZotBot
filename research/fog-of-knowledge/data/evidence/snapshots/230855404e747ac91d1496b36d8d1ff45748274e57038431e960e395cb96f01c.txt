Scope Note
The attempt to improve the
PHENOTYPES
of future generations of the human population by fostering the reproduction of those with favorable phenotypes and
GENOTYPES
and hampering or preventing
BREEDING
by those with undesirable phenotypes and genotypes. The concept is largely discredited. (McGraw-Hill Dictionary of Scientific and Technical Terms, 6th ed)
