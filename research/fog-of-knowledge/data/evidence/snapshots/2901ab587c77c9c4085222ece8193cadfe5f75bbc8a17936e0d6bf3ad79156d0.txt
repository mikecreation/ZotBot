k²-tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
compact
representation of a sparse
directed graph
.  It requires 1 to 3
bits
per edge.  Many access functions, like successor(s), predecessor(s), and range (all links from a range of
vertices
to another range of vertices), have very efficient implementations.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
compact data structure
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
adjacency-matrix representation
,
quadtree
.
Note:
A k²-tree is particularly useful for Web graphs, which are very sparse. This needs (N +
o
(N))/N bits per edge, where N is the number of edges.  Checking if there is an edge from any vertex to another takes
O
(log
k
N), where k is the branching factor.
