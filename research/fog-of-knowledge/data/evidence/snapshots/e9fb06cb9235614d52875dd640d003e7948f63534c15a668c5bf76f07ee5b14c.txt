combination
combination
(algorithm)
Definition:
Choose m of n elements, where m ≤ n.
See also
permutation
,
subset
.
Note:
A combination is a subset with exactly m elements.
There are
n!/(n-m)!m!
combinations of n (distinguishable) elements taken m at a time.  Why?  To begin, there are n!
permutations
.  Permutations that only differ in the order of the n-m elements not taken are the same combination, so divide by (n-m)!.  The order of the m elements taken doesn't matter either, so divide by m!, too.
