skd-tree
(data structure)
Definition:
The spatial
k-d tree
is a
spatial access method
where successive levels are split along different dimensions.  Objects are indexed by their
centroid
, and the
minimum bounding box
of objects in a node are stored in the node.
See also
extended k-d tree
.
Note:
After
[GG98]
.
