Russell’s paradox
Suppose that for any coherent

⁢

Bertrand Russell gave this

An

without any

If you answer himself that is false since he only shaves all those who do not themselves shave. If you answer someone else that is also false because he shaves all those who do not themselves shave and in this case he is part of that set since he does not shave himself. Therefore we have a contradiction.
