PLOP-hashing
(data structure)
Definition:
Piecewise linear order-preserving (PLOP) hashing is a
spatial access method
which splits space into a nonperiodic grid.  Each spatial dimension is divided by nodes of a
binary tree
.  Object are stored in the grid cell of their
centroid
.
See also
grid file
.
Note:
After
[GG98]
.
