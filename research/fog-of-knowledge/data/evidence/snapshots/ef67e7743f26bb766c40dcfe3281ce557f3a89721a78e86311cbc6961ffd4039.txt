3
Motivation
Both of these notions generalise the definition of a group. In particular, a regular semigroup with one idempotent is a group: as such, many interesting subclasses of regular semigroups arise from putting conditions on the idempotents. Apart from inverse semigroups, there are
orthodox semigroups
where the set of idempotents is a subsemigroup, and
Clifford semigroups
where the idempotents are central.
