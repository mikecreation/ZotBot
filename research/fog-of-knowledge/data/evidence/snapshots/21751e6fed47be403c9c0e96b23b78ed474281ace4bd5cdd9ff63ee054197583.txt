Stirling's approximation
Stirling's approximation
(definition)
Definition:
For large values of n,
n!
≈ (n/e)
n
√(2nπ).
See also
factorial
,
gamma function
.
Note:
This approximation is taken directly from
Stirling's formula
.
Author:
PEB
More information
Peter Luschny lists and evaluates
many approximation formulas for n!
. See Eric W. Weisstein,
Stirling's Approximation
for a derivation and other approximations.
