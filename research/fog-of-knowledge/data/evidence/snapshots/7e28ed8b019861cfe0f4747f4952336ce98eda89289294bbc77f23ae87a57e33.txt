total function
total function
(definition)
Definition:
A
function
which is defined for all inputs of the right type, that is, for all of a
domain
.
See also
partial function
.
Note:
Square (x²) is a total function.  Reciprocal (1/x) is not, since 0 is a real number, but has no reciprocal.
