binary tree
binary tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
tree
with at most two
children
for each
node
.
Formal Definition:
A binary tree either
is empty (no nodes), or
has a
root
node, a left binary tree, and a right binary tree.
Also known as
dyadic tree.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
tree
,
k-ary tree
with k=2.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
complete binary tree
,
full binary tree
,
binary search tree
,
binary heap
,
balanced binary tree
,
threaded tree
,
Merkle tree
,
Fibonacci tree
,
extended binary tree
.
Note:
Formal definition after
[CLR90, page 94]
.
