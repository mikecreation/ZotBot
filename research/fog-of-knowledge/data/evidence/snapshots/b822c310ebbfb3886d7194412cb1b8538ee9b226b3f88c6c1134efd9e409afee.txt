doubly linked list
doubly linked list
(data structure)
Definition:
A variant of a
linked list
in which each item has a
link
to the previous item as well as the next.  This allows easily accessing list items backward as well as forward and deleting any item in constant time.
Also known as
two-way linked list, symmetrically linked list.
See also
jump list
.
Note:
See
[Stand98, p. 91]
.
Binary search
may be effective with an
ordered
,
doubly linked list
. It makes O(n) traversals, as does
linear search
, but it only performs O(log n) comparisons.
