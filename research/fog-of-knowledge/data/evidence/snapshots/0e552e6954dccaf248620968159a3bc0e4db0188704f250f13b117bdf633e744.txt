multigraph
multigraph
(data structure)
Definition:
A
graph
whose
edges
are unordered pairs of
vertices
, and the same pair of vertices can be connected by multiple edges.
Formal Definition:
Same as graph, but E is a
bag
of edges, not a
set
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
graph
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
Christofides algorithm
.
See also
hypergraph
.
Note:
A definition of "pseudograph" is a multigraph that may have
self-loops
.
