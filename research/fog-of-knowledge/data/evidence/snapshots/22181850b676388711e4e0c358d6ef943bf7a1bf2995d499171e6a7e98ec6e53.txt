performing arts (discipline)
(arts (broad discipline), <arts and related disciplines>, ... Disciplines (hierarchy name))
Note:
Disciplines concerning arts that depend upon performances presented to the attention of observers through the live or recorded actions of the artists, such as actors, musicians, poets, etc.

Chinese (traditional)

Dutch
..... Kunstvormen die aan de aandacht van waarnemers worden gepresenteerd door middel van rechtstreekse of opgenomen handelingen van de kunstenaars, zoals dansen, acteren, of het uitvoeren van muziek.
Portuguese
..... Disciplinas relativas às artes que dependem de performances apresentadas à atenção dos observadores através de ações ao vivo ou gravadas dos artistas, como atores, músicos, poetas, etc.
Spanish
