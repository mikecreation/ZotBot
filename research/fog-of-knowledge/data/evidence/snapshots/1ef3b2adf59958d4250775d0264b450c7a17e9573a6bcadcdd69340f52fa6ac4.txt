Ford-Fulkerson method
Ford-Fulkerson method
(algorithm)
Definition:
Given a
flow function
and its corresponding residual graph (a
maximum-flow problem
), select a
path
from the
source
to the
sink
along which the
flow
can be increased and increase the flow. Repeat until there are no such paths.
Note:
This algorithm was proposed in 1956.  After Wilf.
