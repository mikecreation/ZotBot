automotive design
(design (discipline), arts-related disciplines, ... Disciplines (hierarchy name))
Note:
Practice and discipline of designing the appearance, ergonomics, complex systems, and other aspects of motor vehicles, usually road vehicles such as automobiles, motorcycles, trucks, buses, coaches, and vans.
