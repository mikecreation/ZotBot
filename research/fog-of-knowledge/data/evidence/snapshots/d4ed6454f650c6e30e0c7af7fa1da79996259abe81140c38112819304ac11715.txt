Definition:
The problem of finding the k
th
shortest path
from one
vertex
in a
graph
to another vertex.  Variants may require that paths are edge- or vertex-disjoint, that is sharing no
edges
or vertices.  "Shortest" may be least number of edges, least total  weight, etc.
