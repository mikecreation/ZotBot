A
semiotic equivalence relation
(SER) is a type of
equivalence relation
that arises in the
analysis
of
sign relations
. Any equivalence relation is closely associated with a corresponding set of
equivalence classes
that
partition
the underlying set of
elements
, commonly called the
domain
or the
space
of the
relation
. In the case of a SER, the equivalence classes are called
semiotic equivalence classes
(SEC’s) and the partition is called a
semiotic partition
(SEP).

A few items of notation are useful in discussing equivalence relations in general and semiotic equivalence relations in particular.
As a general consideration, if
