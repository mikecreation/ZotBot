isomorphic
(definition)
Definition:
Two
graphs
are isomorphic if there  is a one-to-one correspondence between their
vertices
and there is an
edge
between two vertices of one graph if and only if there is an edge between the two corresponding vertices in the other graph.
See also
homeomorphic
.
