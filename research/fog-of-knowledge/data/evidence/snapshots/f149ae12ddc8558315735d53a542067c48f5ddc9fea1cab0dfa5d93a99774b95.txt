partially decidable problem
(definition)
Definition:
One whose associated
language
is a
recursively enumerable language
.  Equivalently, there exists an
algorithm
that halts and outputs 1 for every instance having a "yes" answer, but for instances having a "no" answer is allowed either not to halt or to halt and output 0.
See also
decidable problem
,
undecidable problem
,
solvable
,
tractable
,
computable
.
