heuristic
heuristic
(algorithmic technique)
Definition:
An
algorithm
that usually, but not always, works or that gives nearly the right answer.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
move-to-front heuristic
,
Christofides algorithm
,
Monte Carlo algorithm
.
See also
metaheuristic
,
probabilistic algorithm
,
greedy algorithm
.
Note:
Some problems, such as the
traveling salesman
problem, may take far too long to compute an
optimal solution
.  But some have good heuristics that are fast and find a solution no more than a few percent worse than the optimal.
