semidefinite programming
(definition)
Definition:
A generalization of a
linear program
in which any subset of the variables may be constrained to form a semidefinite matrix.
Note:
Used in recent results and obtaining better approximation algorithms for cut, satisfiability, and coloring problems. From Algorithms and Theory of Computation Handbook, page 34-18, Copyright © 1999 by CRC Press LLC.
