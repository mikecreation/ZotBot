Order Ideals and Filters
Let

↓

•

:

elements
in
I
has an
upper bound
in
I
.
An order ideal is also called an ideal for short. An ideal is said to be

↓

Given a subset

⟩

is a directed set. In particular, for any

∈
P
,
↓

is the ideal generated by

is an

, then for any

⊆

, let

′
be the set of finite joins of elements of

is a directed set, and
⟨

⟩
=
↓

′
.
Dually, an
order filter
(or simply a

is a non-empty subset

which is both an upper set and a filtered set (every pair of elements in

Remark
.
This is a
generalization
of the notion of a filter (
http://planetmath.org/Filter
) in a set. In fact, both ideals and filters are generalizations of ideals and filters in semilattices and lattices.
Examples in a Semilattice
A subset

Then the two definitions are

Going one step further, we see that if

∧

Dually, we can define a

, which is equivalent to an order filter of the underly poset. Going one step futher, we also see that a lattice filter in a lattice is an order filter of the underlying poset.
