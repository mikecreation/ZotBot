halting problem
halting problem
(classic problem)
Definition:
Is there an
algorithm
to determine whether any arbitrary program halts?  Turing proved the answer is, no. Since many questions can be recast to this problem, we can conclude that some programs are absolutely impossible, although
heuristic
or partial solutions are possible.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
undecidable problem
.
See also
uncomputable problem
,
Turing machine
.
Note:
In more detail, can there be a program that, when given any arbitrary program, finishes in finite time and correctly answers "Yes, the program you gave me halts for all inputs" or "No, the program you gave me does not halt for some inputs".
This is an informal wording of what Turing proved.  Please do not refer to this page if you claim to refute his proof.
