finite state transducer
(definition)
Definition:
A
finite state machine
specifically with a read-only input and a write-only output.  The input and output cannot be reread or changed.
See also
Turing transducer
,
pushdown transducer
.
Note:
Thanks to L. J. Buitinck for correcting the definition.  28 May 2004.
