All three fusion products exhibit markedly better agreement with rain\u2010gauge observations than the original satellite and radar products in both temporal evolution and precipitation magnitude, effectively reducing the systematic biases associated with complex terrain. The generalisation capability of the proposed framework was further evaluated using independent observations from April to October 2023 together with two representative heavy rainfall events that occurred over the western hilly region and the northeastern mountainous region, respectively.
