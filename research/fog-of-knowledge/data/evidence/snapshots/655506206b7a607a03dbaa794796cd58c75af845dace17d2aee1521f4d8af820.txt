decision problem
decision problem
(definition)
Definition:
A problem with a "yes" or "no" answer.  Equivalently, a
function
whose
range
is two values, such as {0,1}.
See also
optimization problem
,
certificate
,
NP
,
NP-complete
.
Note:
A decision problem asks, is there a solution with a certain characteristic?  An optimization problem asks, what is the best solution?  For instance, the
traveling salesman
problem is an optimization problem, while the corresponding decision problem asks if there is a
Hamiltonian cycle
with a cost less than some fixed amount k.
