Post-Colonial
(<styles, periods, and cultures by general era>, Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Basic reference to 'after the colonial" condition, periods, and cultures of peoples, states, and societies that have been through a process of formal decolonization. Since the 1980s the term is used to also refer to the trend to question Western knowledge, categories, and assumptions.
