recursion tree
(definition)
Definition:
A method to analyze the
complexity
of an
algorithm
by diagramming the
recursive
function calls.
Formal Definition:
A recursion tree T(p) of degree p is either (i)
null
or (ii) has p
children
which are recursion trees.
Note:
Also known as an "R-tree".
