st-digraph
(data structure)
Definition:
A
directed acyclic graph
with two specially marked nodes, the
source
s and the
sink
t.
See also
flow network
.
Note:
After P. Bertolazzi, G. Di Battista, and W. Didimo,
Quasi-upward planarity
, Algorithmica, 32:474-506 (2002).
