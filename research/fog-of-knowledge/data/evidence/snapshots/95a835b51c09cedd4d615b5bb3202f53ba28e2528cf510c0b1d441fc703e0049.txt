bisector
bisector
(definition)
Definition:
For two elements e
i
and e
j
, the locus of points equidistant from e
i
and e
j
.  That is {p|d(p,e
i
)=d(p(e
j
)}, where d is some distance metric.
Note:
For instance, if e
i
and e
j
are two points in the plane and we use
Euclidean distance
, the bisector is the perpendicular bisector of the line segment  e
i
,e
j
.
