polynomial hierarchy
(definition)
Definition:
The classes of
languages
accepted by k-
alternating Turing machines
, over all k≥ 0 and with initial state existential or universal.  The bottom level (k=0) is the class
P
.  The next level (k=1) comprises
NP
and co-
NP
.
Note:
