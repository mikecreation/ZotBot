complete binary tree
complete binary tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
binary tree
in which every
level
(depth), except possibly the deepest, is completely filled.  At depth n, the
height
of the tree, all
nodes
must be as far left as possible.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
complete tree
,
binary tree
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
binary heap
,
perfect binary tree
.
See also
full binary tree
,
extendible hashing
,
heap
.
Note:
A complete binary tree has 2
k
nodes at every
depth
k < n and between 2
n
and 2
n+1
-1 nodes altogether. It can be efficiently implemented as an
array
, where a node at index i has children at indexes 2i and 2i+1 and a parent at index i/2, with
1-based indexing
.   If child index is greater than the number of nodes, the child does not exist.

perfect binary trees
"complete".
