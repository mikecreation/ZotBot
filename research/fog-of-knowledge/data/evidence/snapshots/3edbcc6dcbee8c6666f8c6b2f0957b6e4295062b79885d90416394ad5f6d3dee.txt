weak-heap
weak-heap
(data structure)
Definition:
A relaxed
heap
satisfying the following three conditions: (1) every
key
in the right subtree of a
node
is greater      than the key stored in the node itself,  (2) the
root
has no left
child
, and (3)
leaves
are only found on the last two
levels
of the tree.
See also
weak-heap sort
.
Note:
A weak-heap may be efficiently implemented with an
array
a of items and an array r of reverse bits.  The left child of an index i is at index 2i+1-r
i
, and the right child is at index 2i+r
i
.
This is a minimum weak-heap.  A maximum weak-heap has subtree keys that are less than the node's key.
