bucket
bucket
(definition)
Definition:
An area of storage where items with a common property are stored. Typically
tree
data structures and
sort
algorithms use many buckets, one for each group of items.  Usually buckets are kept on disk.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
bag
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
radix sort
,
bucket sort
,
elastic-bucket trie
,
hash heap
,
extendible hashing
.
Note:
A
bucket
is used when a number of items need to be kept together, but the order among them is not important. Conceptually it is a
bag
(rather than a
set
).
