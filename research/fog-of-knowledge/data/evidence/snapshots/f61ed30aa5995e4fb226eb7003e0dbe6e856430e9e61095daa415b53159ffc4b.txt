edge coloring
(definition)
Definition:
An assignment of colors (or any distinct marks) to the
edges
of a
graph
.  A coloring is a
proper coloring
if no two
adjacent
edges have the same color.
See also
vertex coloring
,
k-coloring
,
chromatic index
.
