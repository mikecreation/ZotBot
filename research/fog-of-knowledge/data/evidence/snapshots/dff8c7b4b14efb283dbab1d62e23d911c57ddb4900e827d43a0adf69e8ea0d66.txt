top-down radix sort
top-down radix sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A recursive
bucket sort
where elements are distributed based on succeeding pieces (characters) of the
key
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
bucket sort
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
postman's sort
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
recursion
.
See also
radix sort
.
Note:
For the first pass, elements are distributed according to the first character.  In the first recursive call, the elements in each bucket are distributed to new "subbuckets" according to the second character.  Succeeding deeper recursions use corresponding succeeding characters.
This is similar to
radix sort
, but works "top down" or "most significant digit first" while radix sort works "bottom up" or "least significant digit first".  Also radix sort merges distributed items while this does not.
