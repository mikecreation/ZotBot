vertical visibility map
vertical visibility map
(definition)
Definition:
A partition of the plane into regions by drawing a vertical straight line through each
vertex
p of a planar straight-line
graph
until it intersects an
edge
e of the graph or extends to infinity.  The edge e is said to be vertically visible from p.
See also
horizontal visibility map
,
visibility map
,
visible
.
