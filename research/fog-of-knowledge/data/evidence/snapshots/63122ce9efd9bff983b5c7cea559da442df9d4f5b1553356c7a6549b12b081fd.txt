buddy system
buddy system
(algorithm)
Definition:
A memory allocation strategy which recursively divides allocatable
blocks
of memory into pairs of adjacent equal-sized blocks called "buddies".
Note:
This term is included to minimize confusion with a
buddy tree
. After LK.
