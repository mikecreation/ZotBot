parent
parent
(definition)
Definition:
Of a
node
: the
tree
node conceptually above or closer to the
root
than the node and which has a
link
to the node.  See the figure at
tree
.
See also
child
,
ancestor
,
internal node
.
Note:
By definition, a parent node is an
internal node
.
