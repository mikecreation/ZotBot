balanced quicksort
balanced quicksort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A variant of
quicksort
which attempts to choose a pivot likely to represent the middle of the values to be sorted.
See also
median
.
Note:
This is one of several attempts to prevent bad
worst cases
in quicksort by choosing the initial pivot.
