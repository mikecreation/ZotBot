block addressing index
block addressing index
(data structure)
Definition:
An
inverted index
that includes the
block
, or general location, within texts, in addition to the text in which the word appears.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
inverted index
.
See also
full inverted index
,
inverted file index
.
Note:
See the example at
inverted index
.
Author:
PEB

,
Compression: A Key for Next-Generation Text Retrieval Systems
, IEEE Computer, 33(11):37-44, November 2000, (page 42).
