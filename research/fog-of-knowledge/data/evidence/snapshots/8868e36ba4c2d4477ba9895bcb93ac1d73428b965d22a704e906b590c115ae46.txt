bardo
(Buddhist doctrinal concepts, doctrinal concepts, ... Associated Concepts (hierarchy name))
Note:
In Tibetan Buddhism, the intermediate state between death and rebirth. Bardo, which can last up to 49 days, follows a vision of a beam of clear light by the dying person.
