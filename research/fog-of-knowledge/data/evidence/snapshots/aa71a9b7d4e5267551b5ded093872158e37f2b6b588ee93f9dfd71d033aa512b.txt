comb sort
comb sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
An
in-place sort
algorithm that repeatedly reorders different pairs of items.  On each pass swap pairs of items separated by the increment or gap, if needed, and reduce the gap (divide it by about 1.3).  The gap starts at about 3/4 of the number of items.  Continue until the gap is 1 and a pass had no swaps.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
diminishing increment sort
.
See also
bubble sort
,
Shell sort
.
Note:
Bubble sort can be seen as a variant of comb sort where the gap is always 1.  Since items may move large distances at first, comb sort is quite efficient.
Comb sort does a single "bubbling" pass (ala
bubble sort
) over  each set for each gap or increment, whereas
Shell sort
completely sorts each set.   J. Incerpi and R. Sedgewick (1985) point out that bidirectional "bubbling" (ala
bidirectional bubble sort
) is more symmetric than the typical comb sort.
