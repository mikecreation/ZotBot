buddy tree
buddy tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
point access method
which splits multidimensional space in different dimensions at each level.  It also keeps a
minimum bounding box
of points accessible by each node.
Note:
After
[GG98]
.
Author:
PEB

and
Hans-Peter Kriegel
