Remarks
•
Hilbert systems need not be unique for a given logical system. For example, see this link (

•
For a given logical system, every Hilbert system is deductively equivalent to a

•
Since axioms are semantically valid statements, the use of Hilbert systems is more about deriving other semantically valid statements, or

themselves. Outside of structural proof theory, deductive systems a la Hilbert style are used almost exclusively everywhere in mathematics.
