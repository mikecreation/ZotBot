matching
matching
(definition)
Definition:
(1) A
subgraph
in which every
vertex
has a
degree
at most one.  In other words, no two
edges
share a common vertex.   (2) The problem of finding such a subgraph.
See also
string matching
,
perfect matching
,
free edge
,
matched edge
,
free vertex
,
matched vertex
.
