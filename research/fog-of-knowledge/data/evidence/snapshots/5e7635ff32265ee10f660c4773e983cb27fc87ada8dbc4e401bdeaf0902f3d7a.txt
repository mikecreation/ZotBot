transitive reduction
(definition)
Definition:
The transitive reduction of a
directed graph
G is the directed graph G' with the smallest number of
edges
such that for every
path
between
vertices
in G, G' has a path between those vertices.
See also
reduced digraph
,
transitive closure
.
Note:
Informally G' is the minimal graph with the same connectivity as G. After abstract of
A. V. Aho, M. R. Garey
, and
J. D. Ullman
.
The transitive reduction of a directed graph
. SIAM Journal on Computing, 1:131--137, 1972.
