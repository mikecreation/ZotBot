substring
substring
(definition)
Definition:
A
string
v is a substring of a string u if u=u′ vu″  for some
prefix
u′ and
suffix
u″.
Also known as
factor.
See also
subsequence
.
Note:
A substring is contiguous.  A subsequence need not be.
