right-threaded tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A variant of a
threaded tree
in which only the right thread, i.e.
link
to the successor, of each
node
is maintained.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
threaded tree
.
See also
binary search tree
.
Note:
Keeping only the right thread, rather than both left and right threads, keeps it quick to find the next greater key and faster to insert and delete.
