knight's tour
(classic problem)
Definition:
A series of moves of a chess knight that visits all squares on the board exactly once.
See also
Hamiltonian cycle
.
Note:
The associated problem is to find such a series of moves.  The problem can be generalized to an n × m rectangular chess board. Solutions may be found using
backtracking
.
