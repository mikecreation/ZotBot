subgraph

Definition:
A
graph
whose
vertices
and
edges
are
subsets
of another
graph
.
Formal Definition:
A
graph
G'=(V', E') is a subgraph of another graph  G=(V, E) iff
V'⊆ V, and
E'⊆ E ∧ ( (v
1
, v
2
)∈ E'     → v
1
, v
2
∈ V').
Note:
In general, a subgraph need not have all possible edges.  If a subgraph has every possible edge, it is an
induced subgraph
.
