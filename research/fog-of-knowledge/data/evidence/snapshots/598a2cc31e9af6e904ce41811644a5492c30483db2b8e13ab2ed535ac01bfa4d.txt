complete graph
(definition)
Definition:
An
undirected graph
with an
edge
between every pair of
vertices
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
undirected graph
,
dense graph
,
connected graph
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
clique
.
See also
sparse graph
,
complete tree
,
perfect binary tree
.
Note:
A complete graph has n(n-1)/2 edges, where n is the number of vertices.
