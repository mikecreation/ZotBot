calendar queue
calendar queue
(data structure)
Definition:
A fast
priority queue
implementation having N buckets each with width w, or covering w time.  An item with priority p more than current goes in bucket (p/w)%N.   Choose N and w to have few items in each bucket.  Keep items sorted within buckets.  Double or halve N and change w if the number of items grows or shrinks a lot.
See also
bucket sort
.
