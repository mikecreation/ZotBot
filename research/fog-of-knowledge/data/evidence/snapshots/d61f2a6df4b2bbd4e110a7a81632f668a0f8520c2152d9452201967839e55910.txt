Abyssinian
(<African by national designation>, African (general, continental cultures), ... Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Style and culture of the historical group that dwelled in the Horn of Africa from the 11th century, comprising mostly Christian, Semitic-speaking peoples in a territory including Eritrea, Tigray, Gonder, and parts of Gojam, Shewa, and Welo in the territory of the modern nation of Ethiopia.
