∼
(definition)
Definition:
(1) Proportional to.  (2) Asymptotically equal to.  A theoretical measure of the execution of an
algorithm
, usually the time or memory needed, given the problem size n, which is usually the number of items.  Informally, saying some equation f(n) ∼ g(n) means it grows at the same rate as g(n). More formally, it means  lim
x → ∞
f(x)/g(x) = 1.
See also
big-O notation
,
Θ(n)
.
