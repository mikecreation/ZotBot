prune and search
prune and search
(algorithmic technique)
Definition:
Find an
optimal value
by eliminating a constant fraction of remaining objects at each step.  Eliminated objects are guaranteed not to affect the optimal value.  A
logarithmic
number of steps reduces the number of objects to a constant, and a
brute force
approach can then solve it.
Also known as
decimation.

[AS98, page 422]
