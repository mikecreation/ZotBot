internal node
(definition)
Definition:
A
node
of a
tree
that has one or more
child
nodes, equivalently, one that is not a
leaf
.
Also known as
nonterminal node.
See also
parent
,
root
.
Note:
See
[CLR90, page 93]
or
[HS83, page 220]
.
