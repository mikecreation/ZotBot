comparison sort
comparison sort
(definition)
Definition:
Any
sort
algorithm using comparisons between
keys
, and nothing else about the keys, to arrange items in a predetermined order.  An alternative is a
restricted universe sort
such as
counting sort
or
bucket sort
.
See also
quicksort
,
insertion sort
.
