0-ary function
0-ary function
(definition)
Definition:
A
function
that takes no arguments.
Also known as
nullary function.
See also
constant function
,
unary function
,
binary function
,
n-ary function
.
Note:
Pronounced "zero-airy".  By analogy to unary, binary, etc.
A 0-ary or nullary function that is pure (no state or interactions with external variables) must be a constant function.
