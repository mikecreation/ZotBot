octree
octree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
tree
to index three dimensions.  Each node has either eight
children
or no children.
See also
quadtree
,
binary tree
,
BSP-tree
.
Note:
If one views a
binary tree
as partitioning one dimension, a
quadtree
analogously partitions two dimensions, and an octree partitions three.
