leaf
(definition)
Definition:
A
node
in a
tree
without any
children
. See the figure at
tree
.
Also known as
external node, terminal node.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
tree
.
See also
root
,
internal node
.
Note:
Every node in a tree is either a leaf or an internal node.  The root may be a leaf.
