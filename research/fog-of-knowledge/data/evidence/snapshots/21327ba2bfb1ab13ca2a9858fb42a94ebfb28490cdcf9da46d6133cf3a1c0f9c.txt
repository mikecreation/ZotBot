select kth element
select k
th
element
(classic problem)
Definition:
Find the k
th
smallest element of a set.  Two approaches are a modified
distribution sort
or
select and partition
.
Also known as
selection problem, find k
th
least element, k
th
smallest element.
See also
Select
,
MODIFIND
,
Find
,
reservoir sampling
.
Note:
The select-and-partition approximately divides in half the number of elements that must be considered at each step.  The modified distribution sort divides by m the number of elements at each step.  The latter is faster, but the former is more generally applicable.
