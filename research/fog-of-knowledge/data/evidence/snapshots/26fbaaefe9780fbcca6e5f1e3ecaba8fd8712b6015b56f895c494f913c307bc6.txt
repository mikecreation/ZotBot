range sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
bucket sort
where the function to determine the
bucket
is based on the
range
of possible
keys
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
bucket sort
,
distribution sort
.

(... is a kind of me.)
pigeonhole sort
