Boole (1854) defines
universe of discourse
in the following manner:
In every discourse, whether of the mind conversing with its own thoughts, or of the
individual
in his intercourse with others, there is an assumed or expressed limit within which the subjects of its
operation
are confined.
…
Now, whatever may be the extent of the field within which all the objects of our discourse are found, that field may properly be termed the universe of discourse. (Boole 1854/1958, p. 42).
