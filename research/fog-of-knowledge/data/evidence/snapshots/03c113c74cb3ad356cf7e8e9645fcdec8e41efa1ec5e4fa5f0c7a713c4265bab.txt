Euclid's algorithm
(algorithm)
Definition:
An algorithm to compute the
greatest common divisor
of two positive integers.  It is
Euclid(a,b){if (b=0) then return a; else return Euclid(b, a mod b);}
.  The run time complexity is
O((log a)(log b))
bit operations.
Also known as
Euclidean algorithm.
See also
binary GCD
,
extended Euclid's algorithm
,
Ferguson-Forcade algorithm
.
