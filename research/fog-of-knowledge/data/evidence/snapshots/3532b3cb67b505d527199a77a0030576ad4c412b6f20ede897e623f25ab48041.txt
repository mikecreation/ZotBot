DAG shortest paths
(algorithm)
Definition:
Solve the
single-source shortest-path problem
in a
weighted
directed acyclic graph
by 1) doing a
topological sort
on the
vertices
by
edge
so vertices with no incoming
edges
are first and vertices with only incoming
edges
are last, 2) assign an infinite distance to every vertex (dist(v)=∞) and a zero distance to the source, and 3) for each vertex v in sorted order, for each outgoing edge e(v,u), if dist(v) + weight(e) < dist(u), set dist(u)=dist(v) + weight(e) and the predecessor of u to v.
See also
Dijkstra's algorithm
,
Bellman-Ford algorithm
.
