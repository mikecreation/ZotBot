relation
relation
(definition)
Definition:
A computation which takes some inputs and yields an output.  Any particular input may yield different outputs at different times. Formally, a mapping from each element in the
domain
to one or more elements in the
range
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
function
,
binary relation
.
Note:
Cosine is a function, since every angle has a specific cosine. Its inverse cos
-1
(x) is a relation, since a cosine value maps to many (for this relation, infinitely many) angles.
