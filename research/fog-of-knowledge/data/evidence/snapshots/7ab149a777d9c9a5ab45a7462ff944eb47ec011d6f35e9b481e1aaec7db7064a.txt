weighted graph
(definition)
Definition:
A
graph
having a weight, or number, associated with each
edge
.  Some algorithms require all weights to be nonnegative, integral, positive, etc.
Also known as
edge-weighted graph.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
labeled graph
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
weighted, directed graph
.
