strand sort
strand sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
sort
algorithm that works well if many items are in order. First, begin a sublist by moving the first item from the original list to the sublist.  For each subsequent item in the original list, if it is greater than the last item of the sublist, remove it from the original list and append it to the sublist.
Merge
the sublist into a final, sorted list.  Repeatedly extract and merge  sublists until all items are sorted.  Handle two or fewer items as special cases.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
distribution sort
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
J sort
.
See also
selection sort
,
merge sort
,
UnShuffle sort
.
Note:
This works especially well with
linked lists
.
