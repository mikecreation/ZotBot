The first stage is its foundation in 1996 and up to 2005 (the celebration of the millennium of Kazan, development of a methodology for dating cities, establishment of relations with the Russian and international scientific community). Khakimov, the team overcame the ideological dogmas of the Soviet era, reconsidered the role of Volga Bulgaria, the Golden Horde, and the ethnogenesis of the Tatars, and developed an original concept of the Turkic-Tatar community without the dominance of a single cultural component. Having outlined the key achievements of the Institute team, the author draws attention to the current challenges that face historical scholarship.
