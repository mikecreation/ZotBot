Johnson-Trotter
Johnson-Trotter
(algorithm)
Definition:
Generate
permutations
by swapping one adjacent pair of elements at a time.
Also known as
Steinhaus-Johnson-Trotter, Trotter-Johnson.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
permutation
.

,
Gray code

unranking
