stooge sort
stooge sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A terribly inefficient
sort
algorithm that swaps the top and bottom items if needed, then (
recursively
) sorts the bottom two-thirds, then the top two-thirds, then the bottom two-thirds again.
See also
bozo sort
,
bogosort
,
lucky sort
.
