orthogonal lists
(data structure)
Definition:
Lists
that share items, but are structurally independent.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
sparse matrix
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
list
.
Note:
The lists are interweaved through items.  For instance, we may keep people on three orthogonal lists by hair color, gender, and country of residence or data values in a
sparse matrix
on two orthogonal lists by row and column.  Inserting an item means inserting it into each list as appropriate.
