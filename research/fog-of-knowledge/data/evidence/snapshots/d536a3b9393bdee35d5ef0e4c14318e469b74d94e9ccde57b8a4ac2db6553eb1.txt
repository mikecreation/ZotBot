busy beaver
busy beaver
(classic problem)
Definition:
(1) A
Turing machine
with a small number of
states
that halts when started with a blank tape, but writes a huge number of non-blanks or takes a huge number of steps.  (2) The problem of finding the maximum number of non-blanks written or steps taken for any Turing machines with a given number of states and symbols.
Note:
The problem is well-defined but rapidly becomes impractical to determine for even a small number of states and symbols. This problem is related to the
halting problem
since one must determine if a machine is looping or eventually halts.
