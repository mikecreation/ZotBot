pure land
(Buddhist doctrinal concepts, doctrinal concepts, ... Associated Concepts (hierarchy name))
Note:
In Mahayana Buddhism, pure abodes or celestial realms of a buddha or bodhisattva; used for East Asian Buddhism, Taoism, and Bon. For realms both pure and mixed, use "buddha fields."
