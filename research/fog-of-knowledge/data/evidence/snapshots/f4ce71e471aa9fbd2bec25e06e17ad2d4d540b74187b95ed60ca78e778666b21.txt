logarithmic
logarithmic
(definition)
Definition:
(1) Any function that is a constant times the logarithm of the argument: f(x)=c log x. (2) In complexity theory, when the measure of computation, m(n) (usually execution time or memory space), is bounded by a logarithmic function of the problem size, n.  More formally m(n) =
O(log n)
. (3) Sometimes imprecisely used to mean
polylogarithmic
.

.
