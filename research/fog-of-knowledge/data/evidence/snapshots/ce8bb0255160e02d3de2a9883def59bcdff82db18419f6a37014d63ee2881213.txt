breadth-first search
breadth-first search
(algorithm)
Definition:
Any search algorithm that considers neighbors of a
vertex
, that is, outgoing
edges
of the vertex's predecessor in the search, before any outgoing edges of the vertex.  Extremes are searched last.  This is typically implemented with a
queue
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
queue
.
See also
depth-first search
,
best-first search
.
Note:
In a tree, equivalent to a
level-order traversal
.
