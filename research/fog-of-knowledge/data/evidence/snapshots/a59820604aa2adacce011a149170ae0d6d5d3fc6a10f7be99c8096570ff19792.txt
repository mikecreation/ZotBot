period style
(<generic styles, periods, and cultures>, Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Term referring to any generic style, particularly in the context of interior design, characterized by the incorporation of antiques or replicas of antiques of one particular named style, usually styles from 18th and 19th-century Europe and the United States (e.g., American Colonial, Louis XIV, Hepplewhite, etc.).
