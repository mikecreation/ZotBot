Post's correspondence problem
Post's correspondence problem
(classic problem)
Definition:
Given a
set
of pairs of
strings
, find a sequence of pairs such that the concatenation of all first members of the pairs is the same string as the concatenation of all second members. This is an
undecidable problem
.
Also known as
PCP.
Author:
SKS
More information
Emil Post
,
A Variant of a Recursively Unsolvable Problem.
Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society, 52:264-268, 1946.
