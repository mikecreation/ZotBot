AVL tree
AVL tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
balanced
binary search tree
where the
height
of the two subtrees (
children
) of a node differs by at most one. Look-up, insertion, and deletion are
O(log n)
, where n is the number of
nodes
in the tree.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
height-balanced tree
,
balanced binary tree
,
binary search tree
,
red-black tree
(when colored).
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
left rotation
,
right rotation
.
See also
B-tree
,
threaded tree
,
Fibonacci tree
.
Note:
The structure is named for the inventors, Adelson-Velskii and Landis. If necessary, the tree is rebalanced after insertions or deletions using rotations.
After Gary Grubb <
[email protected]
>.
An AVL tree is at least as balanced as a red-black tree.
