African diaspora
(African (general, continental cultures), <styles, periods, and cultures by region>, Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Culture of the body of people who were displaced from their African homeland, and their descendants. Examples include those who traveled in ancient migrations and those who were involuntarily transported due to enslavement.
