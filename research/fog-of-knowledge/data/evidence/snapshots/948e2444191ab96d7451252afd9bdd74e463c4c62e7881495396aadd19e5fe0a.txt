cycle
cycle
(definition)
Definition:
A
path
that starts and ends at the same
vertex
and includes at least one
edge
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
path
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
Hamiltonian cycle
,
Euler cycle
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
graph
.
See also
circuit
(2).
Note:
Also known as "circuit" or "closed path".
A cycle includes vertices other than the start/end at most once.
Having at least one edge means that there are at least two vertices in the path: the start/end and one other.  It also means the path length is at least one.
One way to find a cycle is to do a
depth-first search
, checking for repeated vertices.  One step in finding all cycles is to look for
strongly connected components
.
