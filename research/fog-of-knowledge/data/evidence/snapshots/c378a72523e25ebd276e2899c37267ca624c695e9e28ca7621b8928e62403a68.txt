ragged matrix
ragged matrix
(data structure)
Definition:
A
matrix
having irregular numbers of items in each row.
See also
uniform matrix
,
sparse matrix
.
Note:
An
upper triangular matrix
or a
lower triangular matrix
are usually not thought of as being ragged since the number of items in each row is regular (changing by one from row to row).
For example
1
16
18
9
12
6
1
8
3
0
