Indic
(South Asian, Asian, ... Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Culture and style of a diverse ethnolinguistic collection of peoples predominantly found in South Asia who speak languages belonging to the Indo-Aryan branch of the Indo-European family. As they settled the fertile Indus and Gangetic plains, their semi-nomadic pastoral lifestyle transitioned into intensive iron-age agriculture. They brought with them horses, spoked-wheel chariots, and a highly structured spiritual tradition.
