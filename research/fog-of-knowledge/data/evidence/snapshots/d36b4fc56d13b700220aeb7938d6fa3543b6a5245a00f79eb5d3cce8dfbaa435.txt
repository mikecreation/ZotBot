treesort (1)
treesort (1)
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
sort
algorithm that first builds a
binary search tree
of the
keys
, then accesses the keys with an
in-order traversal
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
sort
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
binary search tree
,
in-order traversal
.
See also
blind sort
,
treesort (2)
,
heapsort
.
Note:
For good performance, use a
balanced binary search tree
.
