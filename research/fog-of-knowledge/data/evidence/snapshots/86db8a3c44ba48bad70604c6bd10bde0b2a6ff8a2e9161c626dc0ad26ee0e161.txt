local optimum
local optimum
(definition)
Definition:
A solution to a problem that is better than all other solutions that are slightly different, but worse than the
global optimum
.
See also
prisoner's dilemma
,
optimization problem
.
Note:
Some search methods may get trapped in a local optimum and miss the
global optimum
.
