undirected graph
undirected graph
(data structure)
Definition:
A
graph
whose
edges
are
unordered
pairs of
vertices
.  That is, each edge connects two vertices.
Formal Definition:
A graph G is a pair (V,E), where V is a set of vertices, and E is a set of edges between the vertices E ⊆ {{u,v} | u, v ∈ V}. If the graph does not allow
self-loops
, adjacency is
irreflexive
, that is E ⊆ {{u,v} | u, v ∈ V ∧ u ≠ v}.
See also
directed graph
,
hypergraph
,
multigraph
.
Note:
An undirected graph may be represented as a directed graph with two directed edges, one "to" and one "from," for each undirected edge.
