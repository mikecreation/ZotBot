nonbalanced merge sort
nonbalanced merge sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
k-way merge sort
in which the number of input and  output data streams is different for any particular  pass.  Typically P input streams are merged and distributed to T output streams on one pass followed by a merge of the T inputs and distribution to P outputs.
See also
merge sort
,
simple merge
,
balanced merge sort
.
