Commentz-Walter
Commentz-Walter
(algorithm)
Definition:
A multiple
string matching
algorithm that compares from the end of the
pattern
, like
Boyer-Moore
, using a
finite state machine
, like
Aho-Corasick
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
string matching
.
Author:
PEB
