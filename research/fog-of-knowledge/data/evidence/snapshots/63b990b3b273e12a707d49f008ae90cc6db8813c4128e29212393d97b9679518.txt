coding tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
full binary tree
that represents a coding, such as produced by
Huffman coding
.  Each
leaf
is an encoded symbol.  The
path
from the
root
to a leaf is its codeword.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
full binary tree
,
trie
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
Huffman coding
.
See also
prefix code
.
