parinirvāṇa
(nirvana, Buddhist doctrinal concepts, ... Associated Concepts (hierarchy name))
Note:
In Buddhism, the final nirvana or nirvana-after-death, which occurs upon the death of the body or ultimate departure from the conventional realm of a person who is an awakened being, having attained nirvana during his or her lifetime.
