cellular automaton
cellular automaton
(definition)
Definition:
Usually a
two-dimensional
organization of simple
finite state machines
whose next
state
depends on their own state and the states of their eight closest neighbors. In general the machines may be arranged in meshes of higher or lower dimension, have larger neighborhoods, or be arbitrarily complex processors.
Note:
Conway's game of Life is probably the most well known instance of a cellular automaton.
