Boyer-Moore
Boyer-Moore
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
string matching
algorithm that compares characters from the end of the
pattern
to its beginning.  When characters don't match, searching jumps to the next possible match: the farthest of a table like that used in the
Knuth-Morris-Pratt algorithm
and the next matching position in the
pattern
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
string matching
.
See also
Boyer-Moore-Horspool
,
Commentz-Walter
handles multiple strings, like
Aho-Corasick
.

After
[Sund98]
.
