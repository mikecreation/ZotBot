Master theorem
Master theorem
(definition)
Definition:
A theorem giving a solution in asymptotic terms for
recurrence relations
of the form T(n) = aT(n/b) + f(n) where a ≥ 1 and b > 1 are constants and n/b means either ⌊ n/b⌋ or ⌈ n/b⌉.
