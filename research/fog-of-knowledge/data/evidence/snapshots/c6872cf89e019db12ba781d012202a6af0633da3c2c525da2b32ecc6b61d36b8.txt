bipartite graph
(definition)
Definition:
An
undirected graph
where
vertices
can be
partition
ed into two sets such that no
edge
connects vertices in the same set.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
layered graph
.
Note:
A bipartite graph is a layered graph with two layers.
