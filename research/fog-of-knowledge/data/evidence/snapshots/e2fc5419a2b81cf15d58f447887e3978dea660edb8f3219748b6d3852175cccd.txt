binomial tree
binomial tree
(data structure)
Definition:
An
ordered tree
of
order
k ≥ 0, that is B
k
, whose
root
has k
children
where the i
th
child is binomial tree of order k-i.
See also
binomial heap
.
Note:
A B
k
tree has 2
k
nodes, the
height
is k, and there are k choose i nodes at depth i.
Adapted from
[CLR90, pages 401 and 402]
. CLR90 numbers the children k-1, k-2, ..., 0, making child i a binomial tree of order i. This definition numbers the children from 1 to k.
