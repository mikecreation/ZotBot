amortized cost
amortized cost
(definition)
Definition:
The theoretical speed of a given set of operations.  It is
O(f(n))
when the execution time of the
worst case
of all sequences of
n
operations never exceeds O(n*f(n)).
See also
potential function
,
best-case cost
,
average-case cost
,
worst-case cost
.
Note:
The term "amortized worst case" is sometimes used to emphasize that the worst case of a set of operations is averaged.
