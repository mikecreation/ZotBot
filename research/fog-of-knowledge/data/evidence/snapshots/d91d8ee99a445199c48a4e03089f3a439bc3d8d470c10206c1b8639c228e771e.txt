minimum spanning tree
minimum spanning tree
(definition)
Definition:
A minimum-weight
tree
in a
weighted graph
which contains all of the graph's
vertices
.
Also known as
MST, shortest spanning tree, SST.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
spanning tree
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
Christofides algorithm
(1).
See also
Kruskal's algorithm
,
Prim-Jarnik algorithm
,
Boruvka's algorithm
,
Steiner tree
,
arborescence
, similar problems:
all pairs shortest path
,
traveling salesman
.
Note:
A minimum spanning tree can be used to quickly find a near-optimal solution to the
traveling salesman
problem.
The term "shortest spanning tree" may be more common in the field of operations research.
A Steiner tree is allowed additional connection points to reduce the total length even more.
