BB(α) tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
binary tree
where the
balance
of every subtree,  ρ(T'), is bounded by α ≤ ρ(T') ≤ 1-α.
Also known as
weight-balanced tree.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
binary tree
,
balanced tree
.
