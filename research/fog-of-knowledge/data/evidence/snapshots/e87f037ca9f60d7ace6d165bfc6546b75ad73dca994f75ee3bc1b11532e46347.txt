bozo sort
bozo sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A terribly inefficient
sort
algorithm that randomly swaps items until they are in order.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
sort
algorithm,
Las Vegas algorithm
.
See also
bogosort
,
stooge sort
,
permutation sort
,
lucky sort
.
Note:
Run time analysis in
Hermann Gruber, Markus Holzer
, and
Oliver Ruepp
,
Sorting the slow way: An analysis of perversely awful randomized sorting algorithms
, FUN 2007, LNCS 4475, pp. 183-197, 2007.
Note: "Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious" is misspelled on the sixth page, although it is spelled correctly in the footnote.
