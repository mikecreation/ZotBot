k-ary Huffman coding
(algorithm)
Definition:
A minimal variable-length coding based on the frequency of each character.  Similar to a
Huffman coding
, but joins k trees into a
k-ary tree
at each step, and uses k symbols for each level.
Note:
The coding at each stage has k symbols, not just 2 (0 or 1) like traditional Huffman.  If k is a power of two, that is, k=2
n
, every symbol can be represented by n bits.
