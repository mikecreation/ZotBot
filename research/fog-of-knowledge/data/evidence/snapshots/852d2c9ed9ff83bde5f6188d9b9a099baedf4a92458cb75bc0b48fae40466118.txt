flow network
flow network
(definition)
Definition:
A
weighted
,
directed graph
with two specially marked nodes, the
source
s and the
sink
t, and a
capacity
function that maps
edges
to positive real numbers, u: E |→ ℜ
+
.
See also
st-digraph
,
maximum-flow problem
,
flow function
,
blocking flow
.
Note:
A graph with multiple sources or sinks may be converted into a flow network by adding a
supersource
, which has infinite capacity to all sources, or a
supersink
, which has infinite capacity from all sinks.
