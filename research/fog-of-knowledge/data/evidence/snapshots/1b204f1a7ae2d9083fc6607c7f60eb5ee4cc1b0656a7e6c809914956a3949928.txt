ordered tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
tree
where the
children
of every
node
are ordered, that is, there is a first child, second child, third child, etc.
Note:
An unordered tree may be thought of as a
recursive
bag
, while an ordered tree is a
recursive
list
.
