primitive algorithm
(definition)
Definition:
An
algorithm
in which all the computable steps are basic operations.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
algorithm
.
See also
compound algorithm
,
primitive recursive
.
Note:
Any
algorithm
is either a
primitive algorithm
or a
compound algorithm
.  The distinction between primitive and compound is somewhat subjective.
