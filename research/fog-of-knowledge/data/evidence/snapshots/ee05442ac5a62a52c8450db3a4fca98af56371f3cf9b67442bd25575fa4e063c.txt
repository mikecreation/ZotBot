Remark
. We refrain from calling a deduction a proof. As we have seen earlier, deductions are mathematical abstractions of proofs. So they are mathematical constructed, viewed either as sequences or trees (of formulas). On the other hand, in the literature, “proof” is usually reserved to be used in the meta-language, as in the “proof” of Fermat’s Last Theorem, etc…
