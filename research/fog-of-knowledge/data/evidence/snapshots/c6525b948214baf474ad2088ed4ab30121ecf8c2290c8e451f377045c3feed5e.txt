approximation algorithm
approximation algorithm
(algorithmic technique)
Definition:
An
algorithm
to solve an
optimization problem
that runs in
polynomial time
in the length of the input and outputs a solution that is guaranteed to be close to the
optimal solution
.  "Close" has some well-defined sense called the
performance guarantee
.
See also
ρ-approximation algorithm
,
absolute performance guarantee
.
