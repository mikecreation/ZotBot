matrix-chain multiplication problem
(classic problem)
Definition:
Given a sequence of matrices such that any
matrix
may be multiplied by the previous matrix, find the best association such that the result is obtained with the minimum  number of arithmetic operations.  One may use
dynamic programming
to find the best association.
