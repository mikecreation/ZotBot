monotone priority queue
monotone priority queue
(data structure)
Definition:
A
priority queue
in which a
key
being inserted is never higher in priority than a previously deleted node.
Note:
The keys deleted from a monotone priority queue form a
monotonically increasing
or
monotonically decreasing
sequence -- hence the name. After LK.
