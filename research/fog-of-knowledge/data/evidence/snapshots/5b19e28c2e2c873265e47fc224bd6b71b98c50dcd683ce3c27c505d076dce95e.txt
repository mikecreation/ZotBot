Fibonaccian search
Fibonaccian search
(algorithm)
Definition:
Search a
sorted array
by narrowing possible locations to progressively smaller intervals.  Begin with two
Fibonacci numbers
, p (F(n)) and q (F(n+1)), such that p < n ≤ q, where n is the size of the array.  The first step checks location p.  The size of the next interval is p, if the key is less than the item at that location, or q-p (F(n-1)) if it is greater.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
Fibonacci number
, search locations are a
Fibonacci tree
.
See also
linear search
,
interpolation search
,
binary search
,
jump search
.
Note:
This is similar to a
binary search
, but only needs subtraction, instead of divide by two or shift right, to compute the next position.
