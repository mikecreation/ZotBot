Fibonacci heap
Fibonacci heap
(data structure)
Definition:
A
heap
made of a
forest
of
trees
.  The
amortized cost
of the operations create, insert a value, decrease a value, find minimum, and merge or join (
meld
) two heaps, is a constant
Θ(1)
.  The delete operation takes
O(log n)
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
heap
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
priority queue
,
Dijkstra's algorithm
.

[CLR90, page 421]
.
