purgatory
(doctrinal concepts, religious concepts, ... Associated Concepts (hierarchy name))
Note:
State or place of spiritual cleansing and purification, allowing a purging of sins. A notable example is in the doctrine of the Roman Catholic Church, where it is defined as  a state where the souls of those who die in grace undergo such punishment as necessary to expiate their unforgiven venial sins before entering heaven.
