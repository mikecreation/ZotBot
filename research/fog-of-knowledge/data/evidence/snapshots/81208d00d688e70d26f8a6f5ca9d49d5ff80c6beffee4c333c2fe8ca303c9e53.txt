treap
treap
(data structure)
Definition:
A
binary search tree
in which
nodes
have another
key
, called the priority.  Operations also keep the nodes
heap ordered
with regard to the priority.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
binary search tree
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
randomized binary search tree
.
Note:
The name comes from "
tree
" and "
heap
".
Some call "randomized binary search trees" treaps, but strictly a treap does not define how priorities are assigned.
