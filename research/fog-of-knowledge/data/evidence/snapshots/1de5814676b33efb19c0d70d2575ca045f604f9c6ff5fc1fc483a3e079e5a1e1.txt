superset
superset
(definition)
Definition:
A
set
S
1
is a superset of another set S
2
if every element in S
2
is in S
1
.  S
1
may have elements which are not in S
2
.
See also
subset
.
Note:
If S
1
is a superset of S
2
, S
2
is a subset of S
1
.
