A
topological space
is said to be
second
if it has a countable basis (
http://planetmath.org/BasisTopologicalSpace
).
It can be shown that a  space is both Lindelöf and
separable
, although the converses fail. For instance, the
lower limit topology
on the real line is both Lindelöf and separable, but not
second countable
.
