Saracenic
(Islamic World, The, <styles, periods, and cultures by region>, Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Historical term used by medieval Christian Europeans for the culture and style of Arab Muslims, and eventually all Muslims across the Middle East, North Africa, and Europe. Rather than referencing a specific ethnic group, the term was a sweeping religious and geopolitical label used throughout the Middle Ages, most notably during the Crusades. The meaning of the term has then shifted dramatically across different historical eras. For the style and culture of Muslim lands use "Islamic (style and culture)."
