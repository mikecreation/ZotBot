reachable
reachable
(definition)
Definition:
A
vertex
v is reachable from another vertex u if there is a
path
of any length from u to v.
Also known as
reachability.
See also
sink
,
source
,
connected graph
.
Note:
Usually applied only to
directed graphs
, since any vertex in a
connected
,
undirected graph
is reachable from any other vertex.
