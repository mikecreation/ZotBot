JSort
JSort
(algorithm)
Definition:
An
in-place sort
that partially orders the
array
twice with
build-heap
, once moving lesser items earlier and once in reverse moving greater items later, then uses
insertion sort
on the nearly-ordered array.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
in-place sort
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
build-heap
,
insertion sort
,
heap
.
See also
sort
,
heapsort
,
J sort
.
Note:
The implementation attributes the algorithm to
Jason Morrison
.

implementation (Java)
