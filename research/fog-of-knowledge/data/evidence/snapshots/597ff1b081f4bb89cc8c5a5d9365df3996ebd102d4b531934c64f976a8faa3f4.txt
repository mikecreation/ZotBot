midrange
midrange
(definition)
Definition:
The sum of the minimum and maximum values, divided by two.
See also
mean
,
median
,
mode
.
Note:
The midrange of 1, 1, 3, 50, and 60 is 30.5. (1 + 60) / 2 = 30.5.
