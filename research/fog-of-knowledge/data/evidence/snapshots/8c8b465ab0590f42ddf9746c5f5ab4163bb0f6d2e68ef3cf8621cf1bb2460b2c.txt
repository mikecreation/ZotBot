rectilinear Steiner tree
(definition)
Definition:
A minimum-length
rectilinear
tree connecting a set of points, called
terminals
, in the plane.  This tree may include points other than the terminals, which are called
Steiner points
.
See also
Steiner tree
,
Euclidean Steiner tree
.
