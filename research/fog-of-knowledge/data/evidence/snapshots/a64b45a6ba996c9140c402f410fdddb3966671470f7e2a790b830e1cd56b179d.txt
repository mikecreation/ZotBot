double hashing
double hashing
(algorithm)
Definition:
A method of
open addressing
for a
hash table
in which a
collision
is resolved by searching the table for an empty place at intervals given by a different hash function, thus minimizing
clustering
.
Also known as
rehashing.
See also
linear probing
,
hash table
.
Note:
Since a different hashing function is used to find a location in case of collision, colliding values should be spread out.  In
linear probing
,
primary clustering
occurs when collisions fill up every space for long stretches.  Even in
quadratic probing
,
secondary clustering
may develop since colliding values follow the same
probe sequence
.
