postman's sort
postman's sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A highly engineered variant of
top-down radix sort
where attributes of the
key
are described so the algorithm can allocate buckets and distribute efficiently.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
top-down radix sort
.
See also
multikey Quicksort
.
Note:
This is the algorithm used by letter-sorting machines in the post office: first states, then post offices, then routes, etc.  Since keys are not compared against each other, sorting time is
O(cn)
, where
c
depends on the size of the key and number of buckets.  You probably used a form  of this if you sorted lots of papers alphabetically: you put each paper in its pile (or bucket), A's, B's, C's, D's, etc., then sort each pile.
This is similar to
radix sort
, but works "top down" or "most significant digit first" while radix sort works "bottom up" or "least significant digit first".  Also this does not merge distributed items while radix sort does.
