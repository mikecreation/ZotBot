height-balanced binary search tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
height-balanced tree
which is also a
binary search tree
. It supports membership, insert, and delete operations in time
logarithmic
in the number of
nodes
in the tree.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
height-balanced tree
,
binary search tree
,
balanced binary tree
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
AVL tree
,
red-black tree
.
