Fibonacci tree
Fibonacci tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A variant of a
binary tree
where a tree of order n (n>1) has a left subtree of order n-1 and a right subtree of order n-2.  An order 0 Fibonacci tree has no
nodes
, and an order 1 tree has 1 node.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
binary tree
,
AVL tree
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
Fibonaccian search
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
Fibonacci number
.
Note:
A Fibonacci tree of order n has F(n+2)-1 nodes, where F(n) is the n
th
Fibonacci number
.  A Fibonacci tree is the most unbalanced
AVL tree
allowed.
