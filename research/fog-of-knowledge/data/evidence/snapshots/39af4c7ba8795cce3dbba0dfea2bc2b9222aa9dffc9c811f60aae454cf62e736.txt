Lotka's law
Lotka's law
(definition)
Definition:
The number of authors making n contributions is about 1/n
a
of those making one contribution, where a is often nearly 2.
See also
Zipf's law
,
Benford's law
,
Bradford's law
.
Note:
A power law; often nearly an inverse square law.
