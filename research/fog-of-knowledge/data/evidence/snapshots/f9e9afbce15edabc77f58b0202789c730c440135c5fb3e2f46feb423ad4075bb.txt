simple path
(definition)
Definition:
A
path
that repeats no
vertex
, except that the first and last may be the same vertex.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
Hamiltonian path
,
cycle
.
See also
enumerate
all simple paths
between two vertices.
Note:
In August 2017 the definition changed to allow the first and last vertex to be the same, consistent with Knuth
[Knuth98, 1:363]
.  Before, the definition was "A path that repeats no vertex."
