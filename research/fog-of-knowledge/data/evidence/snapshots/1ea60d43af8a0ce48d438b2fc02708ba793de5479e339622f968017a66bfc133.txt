sparsification
sparsification
(algorithmic technique)
Definition:
Technique for designing dynamic
graph
algorithms
, which when applicable transform a time bound of T(n,m) onto O(T(n,n)), where m is the number of
edges
and n is the number of
vertices
of the given graph.
Note:
From Algorithms and Theory of Computation Handbook, page 8-22, Copyright © 1999 by CRC Press LLC. Appearing in the Dictionary of Computer Science, Engineering and Technology, Copyright © 2000 CRC Press LLC.
