recursive
recursive
(data structure)
Definition:
(1) A data structure that is partially composed of other instances of the data structure.  For instance, a
tree
is composed of smaller trees (
subtrees
) and
leaf
nodes
, and a
list
may  have other lists as elements.  (2) An
algorithm
in which functions might call themselves.  For instance,
quicksort
or
heapify
.
See also
recursion
.
Note:
Infinite data structures may be represented by having a tree include (point back to) itself recursively, a list include itself, etc.  Recursive data structures are often best handled with a recursive algorithm, or an algorithm using
recursion
.
