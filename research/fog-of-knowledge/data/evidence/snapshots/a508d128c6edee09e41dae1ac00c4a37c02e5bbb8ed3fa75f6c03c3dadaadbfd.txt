fully polynomial approximation scheme
(algorithmic technique)
Definition:
A set of
algorithms
{A
ε
| ε > 0},  where each A
ε
is a 1+ε-
approximation algorithm
bounded by a
polynomial
in the length of the input and 1/ε.
See also
polynomial approximation scheme
.
