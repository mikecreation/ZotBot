Zeller's congruence
Zeller's congruence
(algorithm)
Definition:
An algorithm to find the day of the week for any date.
See also
Doomsday rule
.
Author:
PEB

The Calendrical Works of Rektor Chr. Zeller: The Day-of-Week and Easter Formulae

Zeller's congruence, for day-of-week (Pascal, JavaScript)

question 17.28 (C)
