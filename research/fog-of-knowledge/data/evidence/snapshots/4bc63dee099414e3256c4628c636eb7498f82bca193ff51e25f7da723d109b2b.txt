strongly NP-hard
(definition)
Definition:
The
complexity class
of
decision problems
which are still
NP-hard
even when all numbers in the input are bounded by some
polynomial
in the length of the input.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
NP-hard
.
See also
NP-complete
,
NP
.
