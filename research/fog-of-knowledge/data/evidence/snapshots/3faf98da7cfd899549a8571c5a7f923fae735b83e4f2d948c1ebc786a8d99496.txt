            <abstract>We propose a coupling-aware tri-hybrid precoding architecture for simultaneous wireless information and power transfer (SWIPT) using reconfigurable parasitic antennas. By integrating multiport network theory and information theory, we develop a third precoding layer that explicitly accounts for mutual coupling and reactive energy storage within the antenna domain. To efficiently optimize this architecture, we propose a low-complexity tri-hybrid solution comprising coordinate descent (CD) for parasitic reactive load optimization, a singular value decomposition (SVD)-based analog precoder, and a protective partial zero-forcing (PPZF) digital precoder. Numerical results demonstrate that the transmit array introduces impedance mismatch that reduces the real power accepted by the antenna array. Moreover, the proposed tri-hybrid architecture significantly outperforms hybrid precoding by constructively exploiting the coupling effects, yielding substantial gains in spectral efficiency (SE) and sum non-linear harvested energy (NLHE) at a fraction of the hardware cost.</abstract>
