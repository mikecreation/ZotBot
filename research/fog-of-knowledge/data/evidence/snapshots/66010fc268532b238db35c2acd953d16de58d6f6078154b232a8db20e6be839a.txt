compact DAWG
compact DAWG
(data structure)
Definition:
A
directed acyclic
word graph (DAWG) representing the
suffixes
of a given
string
in which each
edge
is labeled with the longest possible string.  The strings along a
path
from the
root
to a
node
are the
substring
which the
node
represents.
See also
directed acyclic word graph
,
Patricia tree
.
Note:
This is a variant of a directed acyclic word graph, or DAWG, in which a
node
with one
child
is merged with its
parent
and the edge labels are concatenated.  A Patricia tree merges single-child nodes also, but does not combine common subtrees.
Author:
PEB
More information
Andrew W. Appel and Guy J. Jacobson
,
The World's Fastest Scrabble Program
, CACM, 31(5):572-578, May 1988. Good description of the basic DAWG.
