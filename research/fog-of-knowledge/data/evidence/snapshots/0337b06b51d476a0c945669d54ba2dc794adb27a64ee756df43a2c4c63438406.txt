adaptive Huffman coding
adaptive Huffman coding
(algorithm)
Definition:
A near-minimal variable-length character coding that changes based on the frequency of characters processed.  As characters are processed, frequencies are updated and codes are changed (or, the coding tree is modified).
Also known as
dynamic Huffman coding.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
Huffman coding
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
Vitter's algorithm
,
algorithm FGK
.
Note:
The total message length can be less than that produced by a static Huffman coding since the coding can be different at different places in the message.
