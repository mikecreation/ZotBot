Zhu-Takaoka
Zhu-Takaoka
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
string matching
algorithm that is a variant of the
Boyer-Moore
algorithm.  It uses two consecutive text characters to compute the bad character shift.  It is faster when the
alphabet
or
pattern
is small, but the skip table grows quickly, slowing the pre-processing phase.
Author:
BB
Implementation
explanation, animation, and example (C)
More information
R. F. Zhu and T. Takaoka
,
On improving the average case of the Boyer-Moore string matching algorithm
, J. Inform. Process. 10(3):173-177 (1987).
