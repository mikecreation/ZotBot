order
order
(definition)
Definition:
(1) The
height
of a
tree
.   (2) The number of
children
of the
root
of a
binomial tree
. (3) The maximum number of children of
nodes
in a
B-tree
. (4) The number of data streams, usually denoted ω, in a
multiway merge
.
See also
total order
,
partial order
.
