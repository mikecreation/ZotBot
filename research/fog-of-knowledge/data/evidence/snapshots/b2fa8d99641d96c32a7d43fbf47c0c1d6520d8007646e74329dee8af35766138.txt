jump search
jump search
(algorithm)
Definition:
Search a
sorted array
by checking every j
th
item until the right area is found, then doing a
linear search
.  The optimum for n items is when j=√ n.
Also known as
block search.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
search
.
See also
interpolation search
,
binary search
,
linear search
.
Note:
If there will be two levels of jumping, the optimum is  j=n
2/3
for the first jumps and j=n
1/3
for the second level of jumps.
Author:
PEB
More information
Ben Shneiderman
,
Jump Searching: A Fast Sequential Search Technique
, CACM, 21(10):831-834, October 1978.
