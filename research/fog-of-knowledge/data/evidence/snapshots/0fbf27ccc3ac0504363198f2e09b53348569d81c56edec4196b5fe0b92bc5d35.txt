dual-pivot quicksort
dual-pivot quicksort
(algorithm)
Definition:
Pick two elements from the
array
to be sorted (the pivots), partition the remaining elements into (i) those less than the lesser pivot, (ii) those between the pivots, and (iii) those greater than the greater pivot, and recursively sort these partitions.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
in-place sort
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
partition
(2).
See also
quicksort
.
Note:
Dual-pivot quicksort is consistently faster than
quicksort
in practice, although classical analysis suggests that it should be slower.  Sebastian Wild offers a plausible explanation why in
Sebastian Wild
,
Why Is Dual-Pivot Quicksort Fast?
,
arXiv:1511.01138
v2 28 Sep 2016.
