1-based indexing
1-based indexing
(definition)
Definition:
Indexing (an
array
) beginning with 1.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
array
.
See also
0-based indexing
.
Note:
That is, an array A with n items is accessed as  A[1], A[2], …, A[n].
