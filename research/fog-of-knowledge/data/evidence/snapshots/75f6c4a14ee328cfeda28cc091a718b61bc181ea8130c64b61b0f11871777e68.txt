capacity constraint
capacity constraint
(definition)
Definition:
The property that the flow on every
edge
of a
flow network
is no more than the edge's
capacity
.  More formally, for all edges e, f(e) ≤ u(e), where f(e) is the flow on e and u(e) is its capacity.
See also
flow network
.
Note:
