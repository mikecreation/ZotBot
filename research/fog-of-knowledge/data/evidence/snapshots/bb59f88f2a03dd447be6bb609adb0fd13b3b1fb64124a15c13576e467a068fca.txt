bipartite matching
bipartite matching
(definition)
Definition:
(1) A
perfect matching
between
vertices
of a
bipartite graph
, that is, a
subgraph
that pairs every vertex with exactly one other vertex. (2) The problem of finding such a matching.
Also known as
maximum bipartite matching.
