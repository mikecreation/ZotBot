Levenshtein distance
Levenshtein distance
(definition)
Definition:
(1) The smallest number of insertions, deletions, and substitutions required to change one
string
or
tree
into another.  (2) A
Θ(m × n)
algorithm to compute the distance between strings, where m and n are the lengths of the strings.
Also known as
edit distance.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
string matching with errors
.
Aggregate child
(... is a part of or used in me.)
edit operation
.
See also
double metaphone
,
soundex
,
Jaro-Winkler
,
Hamming distance
.
