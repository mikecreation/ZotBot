symmetric
symmetric
(definition)
Definition:
A
binary relation
R for which a R b implies b R a.
See also
reflexive
,
transitive
,
antisymmetric
.
Note:
The relation "not equal to" is symmetric: if a ≠ b, then b ≠ a.  However, "loves" is not symmetric since love might not be reciprocated.  The relation "is married to" is symmetric, but not
antisymmetric
: if Paul is married to Marlena, then Marlena is married to Paul (symmetric), but that does not mean that Paul and Marlena are the same person.
