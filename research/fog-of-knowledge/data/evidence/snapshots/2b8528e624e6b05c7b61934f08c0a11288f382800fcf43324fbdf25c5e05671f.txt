sift up
sift up
(algorithm)
Definition:
Restoring the
heap property
by swapping a
node
with its
parent
, and repeating the process on the parent until the
root
is reached or the heap property is satisfied.
Note:
Insertion in a
binary heap
is implemented by adding a node to the end of of the array and then performing a "sift up" starting at that node. After LK.
