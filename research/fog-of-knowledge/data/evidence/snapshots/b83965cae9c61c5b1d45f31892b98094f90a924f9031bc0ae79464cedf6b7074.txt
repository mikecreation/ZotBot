Hispanic-American
(The Americas, <styles, periods, and cultures by region>, Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Refers to the cultures and styles of the countries and communities in South America, North America, Central America, and the Caribbean where Spanish is the official language. In the United States the term Hispanic American also refers to the culture of people from the United States with Latin American or Spanish heritage.
