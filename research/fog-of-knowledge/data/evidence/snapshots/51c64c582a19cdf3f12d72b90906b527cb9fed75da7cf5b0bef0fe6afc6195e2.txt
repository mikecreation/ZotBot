first-in, first-out
first-in, first-out
(definition)
Definition:
A policy that items are processed in order of arrival.  A
queue
implements this.
See also
last-in, first-out
.
Note:
Same as
first come, first served
.  A
breadth-first search
checks newly encountered nodes first-in, first-out. Often written as FIFO.
