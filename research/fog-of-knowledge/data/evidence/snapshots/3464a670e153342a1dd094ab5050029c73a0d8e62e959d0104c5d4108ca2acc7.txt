Jaro-Winkler
Jaro-Winkler
(algorithm)
Definition:
A measure of similarity between two
strings
.  The Jaro measure is the weighted sum of percentage of matched characters from each file and transposed characters.  Winkler increased this measure for matching initial characters, then rescaled it by a piecewise function, whose intervals and weights depend on the type of string (first name, last name, street, etc.).
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
string matching with errors
.
See also
Levenshtein distance
,
phonetic coding
.
Note:
For "piecewise function", see the
definition in MathWorld
.
Author:
PEB

SecondString (Java)
package.
