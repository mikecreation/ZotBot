simple merge
simple merge
(algorithm)
Definition:
Merge
n sorted streams into one output stream. All the stream heads are compared, and the head with the least
key
is removed and written to the output. This is repeated until all streams are empty.
See also
ideal merge
,
optimal merge
.
Note:
The run time is
Θ
(mn), where m is the total number of elements and n is the number of streams.
