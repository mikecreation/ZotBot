optimal
optimal
(definition)
Definition:
(1) A solution to an
optimization problem
which has the minimum (or maximum) value of the
objective function
.  (2) The time, space, resource, etc.
complexity
of an
algorithm
which matches the best known
lower bound
of a problem.
