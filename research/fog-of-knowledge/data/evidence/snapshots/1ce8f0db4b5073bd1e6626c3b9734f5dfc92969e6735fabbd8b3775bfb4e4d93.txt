Steiner point
Steiner point
(definition)
Definition:
(1) A point that is not part of the input set of points, for instance, a point computed to construct a
Steiner tree
.  (2) A point with a particular geometric relation to a triangle.
Note:
Also called a
Steiner vertex
.
Consider the three points of a triangle.  The shortest tree connecting all three, called the
Steiner tree
, branches out from a fourth point in the middle.  That middle point, which was not given initially and must be computed in order to build the shortest tree, is a Steiner point.
