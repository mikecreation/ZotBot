quick search
quick search
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
string matching
algorithm that compares characters from the end of the search string to its beginning.  When a character doesn't match, the next character in the text beyond the search string determines where the next possible match begins.
See also
optimal mismatch
,
Boyer-Moore
.
Note:
After
[Sund98]
.
