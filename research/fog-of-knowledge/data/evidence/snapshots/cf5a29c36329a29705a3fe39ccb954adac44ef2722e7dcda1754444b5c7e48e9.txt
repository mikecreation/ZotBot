randomized binary search tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
binary search tree
in which
nodes
have a randomly assigned priority.  Updates keep priorities in
heap
order instead of keeping
balance
information and doing
rebalance
operations.
Also known as
cartesian tree, RBST.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
treap
.
See also
randomized search tree
.
Note:
Also called a treap, but strictly a treap does not define how priorities are assigned.
Because of the random priority, the trees are always expected to be nearly balanced, regardless of the number, kind and order of updates made.   The expected
worst case
is
O(log n)
for any update or search in a RBST of size n.
