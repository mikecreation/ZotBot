recurrence relation
recurrence relation
(definition)
Definition:
The specification of a sequence of values in terms of earlier values in the sequence and base values.
Also known as
recurrence equations.
See also
Master theorem
.
Note:
Fibonacci numbers
may be described by the recurrence relation F(n) = F(n-1) + F(n-2), where F(1)=1 and F(2)=1.   Execution times are often computed by setting up, then solving, a unary recurrence relation, such as T(n) = 2T(n/4) + 2.
