tabulation hashing
(algorithm)
Definition:
The hash value is the
xor
of values from tables (
arrays
) indexed by characters.
Formal Definition:
h(c) = ⊕
n
i=1
T
i
[c
i
], where ⊕ is the
xor
operation, T
i
is the i
th
table, and c
i
is the i
th
character.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
hash function.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
twisted tabulation hashing
.
See also
Pearson's hash
,
Zobrist_hashing [Wikipedia]
.
Note:
The typical hash function is the xor of the representation of characters, that is, h(c) = ⊕
n
i=1
c
i
.
The tables may be filled with random values or may have values chosen to avoid
collisions
or for other properties.
For games, e.g. chess, "characters" are pieces, like pawn, knight, and queen.
