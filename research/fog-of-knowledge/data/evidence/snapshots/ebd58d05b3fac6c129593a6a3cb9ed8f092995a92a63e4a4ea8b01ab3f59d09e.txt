digital search tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
trie
which stores the
strings
in
internal nodes
, so there is no need for extra
leaf
nodes to store the strings.
See also
digital tree
,
directed acyclic word graph
,
compact DAWG
,
Patricia tree
,
suffix tree
.
