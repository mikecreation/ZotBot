probabilistically checkable proof
(definition)
Definition:
An
interactive proof system
in which provers follow a fixed strategy, that is, one not affected by any messages from the verifier.  The prover's strategy for a given instance x of a
decision problem
can be represented by a finite oracle language B
x
, which constitutes a proof of the correct answer for x.
Note:
