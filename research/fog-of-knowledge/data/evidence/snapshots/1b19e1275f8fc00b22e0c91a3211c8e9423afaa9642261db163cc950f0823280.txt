globalism
(<philosophical movements and attitudes>, philosophical concepts, Associated Concepts (hierarchy name))
Note:
The approach to social, cultural, scientific, ecological, or humanistic questions involving an orientation to the world as a single interacting system, and the placing of its interests above those of individual regions or nations. For the similar theory or belief discussed or applied specifically in the context of politics and political science, use "internationalism."
