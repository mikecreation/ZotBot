heapify
heapify
(algorithm)
Definition:
Rearrange a
heap
to maintain the
heap property
, that is, the
key
of the
root
node
is more extreme (greater or less) than or equal to the keys of its
children
.  If the root node's key is not more extreme, swap it with the most extreme child key, then
recursively
heapify that child's subtree.  The child subtrees must be heaps to start.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
heapsort
.
See also
binary heap
,
build-heap
.
Note:
For an array implementation, heapify takes
O(log
2
n)
or O(h) time under the comparison model, where n is the number of nodes and h is the
height
.
