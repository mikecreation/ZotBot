Round Style
(African sculpture styles, African (general, continental cultures), ... Styles and Periods (hierarchy name))
Note:
Style of African sculpture, characteristically naturalistic, gentle, and organic, featuring rounded shapes, and giving the impression of flesh over the bone structure.
