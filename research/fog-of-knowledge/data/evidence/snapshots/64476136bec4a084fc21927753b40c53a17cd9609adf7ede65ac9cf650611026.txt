extended binary tree
extended binary tree
(data structure)
Definition:
A
binary tree
with special
nodes
replacing every
null
subtree.  Every regular node has two
children
, and every special node has no children.
Note:
After
[Knuth97, 1:399, Sect. 2.3.4.5]
.
