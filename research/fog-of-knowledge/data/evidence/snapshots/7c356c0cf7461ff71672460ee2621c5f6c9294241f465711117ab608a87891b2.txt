R-tree
R-tree
(data structure)
Definition:
(1) A
spatial access method
that splits space with hierarchically nested, and possibly overlapping, boxes.  The tree is height-balanced. (2) A
recursion tree
.
See also
B-tree
,
P-tree
(2),

-tree
,
