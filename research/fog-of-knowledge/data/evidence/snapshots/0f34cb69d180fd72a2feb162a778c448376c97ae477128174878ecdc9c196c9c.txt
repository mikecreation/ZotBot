Abstract

We combine datasets from the first and second science campaigns resulting in a total exposure of 3.1 tonne-years.

Summary and outlook—
In summary, we have performed a blind analysis of a combined SR0+SR1 dataset from XENONnT with a total of 186.5 (95.1) days of live time in SR1 (SR0), resulting in a total exposure of 3.1 tonne-year.
We found no significant excess above background and placed new limits on the SI WIMP-nucleon interaction cross section, with an improvement of approximately a factor of 1.5 for WIMP masses above 30
