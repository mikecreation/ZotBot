xor
xor
(definition)
Definition:
"Exclusive OR" or "not equal to" function:  0 XOR 0 = 0, 0 XOR 1 = 1, 1 XOR 0 = 1, 1 XOR 1 = 0.
Also known as
exclusive or.
See also
and
,
or
,
not
,
implies
.
Note:
The name "exclusive or" is in contrast with inclusive
or
.
