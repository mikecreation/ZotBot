A

of a group

a
quotient
(
http://planetmath.org/QuotientGroup
) of a

G

That is, a section of
G

H

N
,
where
H

G

N

normal subgroup
of
H
.
A group

is

K
.
The

‘is involved in’ is
transitive

http://planetmath.org/Transitive3
),
that is, if
G
is involved in
K
and
K
is involved in
L
,
then
G
is involved in
L
.
Intuitively, ‘
G
is involved in
K
’
means that all of the
structure
of
G
can be found inside
K
.
