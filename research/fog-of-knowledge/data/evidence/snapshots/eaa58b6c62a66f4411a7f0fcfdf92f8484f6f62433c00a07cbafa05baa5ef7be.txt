Theta
Θ
(definition)
Definition:
A theoretical measure of the execution of an
algorithm
, usually the time or memory needed, given the problem size n, which is usually the number of items.  Informally, saying some equation f(n) = Θ (g(n)) means it is within a constant multiple of g(n).  The equation is read, "f of n is theta g of n".
Formal Definition:
f(n) = Θ (g(n)) means there are positive constants c
1
, c
2
, and k, such that  0 ≤ c
1
g(n) ≤ f(n) ≤ c
2
g(n) for all n ≥ k. The values of c
1
, c
2
, and k must be fixed for the function f and must not depend on n.
Also known as
asymptotically tight bound, theta.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
big-O notation
.
See also
∼
.
Note:
This is the upper-case Greek letter Theta.
