oracle Turing machine
(definition)
Definition:
A
Turing machine
with an extra
oracle tape
and three extra states q
?
, q
y
, q
n
.  When the machine enters q
?
, control goes to state q
y
if the oracle tape content is in the
oracle set
; otherwise control goes to state q
n
.
See also
alternating Turing machine
,
nondeterministic Turing machine
,
probabilistic Turing machine
,
universal Turing machine
.
