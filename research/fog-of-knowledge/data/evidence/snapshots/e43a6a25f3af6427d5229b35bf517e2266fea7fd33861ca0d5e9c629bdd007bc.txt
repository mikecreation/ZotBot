insertion sort
insertion sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
Sort by repeatedly taking the next item and inserting it into the final data structure in its proper order with respect to items already inserted.  Run time is
O(n
2
)
because of moves.
Also known as
linear insertion sort.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
sort
.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
binary insertion sort
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
q sort
.
See also
gnome sort
.
Note:
Sorting can be done
in place
by moving the next item into place by repeatedly swapping it with the preceding item until it is in place - a
linear search
and move combined.   This implementation is given in
C. J. Shaw and T. N. Trimble
,
Algorithm 175 Shuttle Sort
, CACM, 6(6):312-313, June 1963.
If comparing items is very expensive, use
binary search
to reduce the number of comparisons needed to find where the item should be inserted, then open a space by moving all later items down one. However a binary search is likely to make this not a
stable
sort.
