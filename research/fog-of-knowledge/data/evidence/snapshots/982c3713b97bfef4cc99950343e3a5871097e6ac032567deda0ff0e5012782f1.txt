spiral storage
spiral storage
(data structure)
Definition:
A
dynamic hashing
table that grows a few slots at a time.  It uses a
hash function
, h, with a
range
of [0,1).  For a key, k, an intermediate value, x=⌈ S-h(k)⌉ +h(k), is computed to find the final slot, ⌊ d
x
⌋, where d>1 is called the growth factor.  To increase the number of slots, increase S to S' and rehash any keys from  ⌊ d
S
⌋ to ⌊ d
S'
⌋-1.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
dynamic hashing
.
See also
linear hashing
.
Note:
Choosing d=2 and S=log
2
N, the number of slots, every expansion retires one slot and creates two new slots.  Since slots in use go from ⌊ d
S
⌋ to  ⌊ d
S+1
⌋-1, they are usually remapped to physical storage.
