clique
clique
(definition)
Definition:
A set of
vertices
in an
undirected graph
in which there is an
edge
between every pair of vertices. In other words, a subgraph that is
complete
.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
complete graph
.
See also
clique problem
.
Note:
Standard pronunciation should be "cleek", to rhyme with antique, boutique, critique, oblique, physique, pique, technique, unique, etc.
Definition after Lior Malka, 31 July 2003.
