probabilistic Turing machine
probabilistic Turing machine
(definition)
Definition:
A
Turing machine
in which some transitions are random choices among finitely many alternatives.
See also
alternating Turing machine
,
nondeterministic Turing machine
,
oracle Turing machine
,
universal Turing machine
,
NP
,
RP
,
ZPP
,
BPP
.
Note:
The typical, deterministic Turing machine (TM) can be seen as a probabilistic TM with no more than one alternative for each transition.   A
nondeterministic TM
is a probabilistic TM ignoring the probabilities.
