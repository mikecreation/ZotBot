homeomorphic
homeomorphic
(definition)
Definition:
Two
graphs
are homeomorphic if they can be made
isomorphic
by inserting new
vertices
of
degree
2 into
edges
.
Note:
This corresponds to the topological notion since adding or removing degree 2 vertices doesn't change the graph's topology.
This is similar to, but not the same as, homomorphic.
