balanced merge sort
(algorithm)
Definition:
A
k-way merge sort
in which the number of input and  output data streams is the same.  See
balanced k-way merge sort
.
See also
merge sort
,
simple merge
,
nonbalanced merge sort
.
Note:
This is an
external sort
.
