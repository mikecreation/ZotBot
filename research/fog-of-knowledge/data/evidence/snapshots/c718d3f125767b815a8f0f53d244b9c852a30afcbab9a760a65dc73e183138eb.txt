stable
stable
(definition)
Definition:
An
algorithm
where the relative order upon input of items with equal
keys
is always preserved in the output.  Usually a
sort
algorithm.
Note:
Many different operations, such as
sort
,
merge
, and select the minimum using a
priority queue
, can be stable.
Suppose we need to order the following people by age:  Max, age 45, Brodsky (19), David (21), Carla (45), and Liang (19). A stable sort would yield Brodsky (19), Liang (19), David (21), Max (45), and Carla (45).  Note that Brodsky still precedes Liang, and Max still precedes Carla.  A sort that is not stable may put Liang before or after Brodsky and Carla before or after Max.  A
radix sort
requires each phase to be stable.
Many algorithms can be turned into a stable variant by appending the original position to the
key
.  When otherwise-equal keys are compared, the positions "break the tie" and the original order is maintained.
