optimal merge
(algorithm)
Definition:
Merge
n sorted sequences of different lengths into one output while minimizing reads.  Only two sequences can be merged at once.  At each step, the two shortest sequences are merged.
Formal Definition:
Let D={n
1
, … , n
k
} be the set of lengths  of sequences to be merged.  Take the two shortest sequences,  n
i
, n
j
∈ D,  such that n≥ n
i
and n≥ n
j
∀ n∈ D.   Merge these two sequences.  The new set D is D' = (D - {n
i
, n
j
}) ∪ {n
i
+n
j
}.   Repeat until there is only one sequence.

Huffman coding
