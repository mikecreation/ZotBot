Hamiltonian path
(definition)
Definition:
A
simple path
through a
graph
that includes every vertex in the graph.
Generalization
(I am a kind of ...)
simple path
.
See also
Hamiltonian cycle
.
Note:
Named for
Sir William Rowan Hamilton (1805-1865)
