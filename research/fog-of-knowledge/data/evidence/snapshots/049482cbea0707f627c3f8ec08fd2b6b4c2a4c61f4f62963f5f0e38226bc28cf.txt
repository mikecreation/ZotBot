kth order Fibonacci numbers
(definition)
Definition:
A sequence of numbers where each number is the sum of the
k
preceding numbers. The usual
Fibonacci numbers
occur when
k=2
.
Formal Definition:
F
(k)
n
=
F
(k)
n-1
+
F
(k)
n-2
+ . . . +
F
(k)
n-k
for  n ≥ k.
F
(k)
k-1
= 1
F
(k)
n
= 0      for    0 ≤ n ≤ k-2
Also known as
pth order Fibonacci numbers.
See also
optimal polyphase merge
.
