depth
depth
(definition)
Definition:
Of a
node
, the distance from the node to the
root
of the
tree
.
Formal Definition:
The depth of the root is 0.  The depth of a node is one plus the depth of its
parent
.
Aggregate parent
(I am a part of or used in ...)
tree
.
See also
height
,
level
.
Note:
For instance, the root has or is at depth 0, and a
child
of the root has or is at depth 1.
