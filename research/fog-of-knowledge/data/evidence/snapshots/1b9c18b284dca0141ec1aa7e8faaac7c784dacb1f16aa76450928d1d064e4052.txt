persistent data structure
persistent data structure
(definition)
Definition:
A
data structure
that preserves its old versions, that is, previous versions may be queried in addition to the latest version.
Specialization
(... is a kind of me.)
partially persistent data structure
,
fully persistent data structure
,
confluently persistent data structure
.
