nor
nor
(definition)
Definition:
Negated disjunction: 0 NOR 0 = 1, 0 NOR 1 = 0, 1 NOR 0 = 0, 1 NOR 1 = 0.
Note:
NOR is equivalent to OR followed by NOT.  It is of interest because all possible
boolean functions
can be built with combinations of NORs.
