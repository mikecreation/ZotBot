# Explainer Video Script

## 0-7 s
**The Not-So-Hard Problem of Consciousness**  
What if the hard problem begins with a definition error?

## 7-14 s
One word has been used for many different variables: biological continuity, wakefulness, responsiveness, memory, report, intelligence, and subjective experience.

## 14-21 s
Anesthesia exposes the split. The biological individual can continue while responsiveness is zero and experience can be present, absent, or unknown.

## 21-28 s
So “Why did a dream happen?” is a real question about phenomenal experience. It is not the same question as “Did the biological individual continue?”

## 28-35 s
SCTC fixes the system-level target: `C_B(t) := L_B(t)`. Everything else gets its own coordinate and must be established independently.

## 35-42 s
The theory can lose. Compare `C := E` against `C := Γ`, and search for an independent system-level variable that SCTC cannot absorb.
