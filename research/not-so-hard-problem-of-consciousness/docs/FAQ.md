# FAQ / Reviewer Firewall

## “You still have not explained why a dream is experienced.”
Correct. That is a question about phenomenal experience `E`. SCTC does not claim to solve `E`. The question becomes an objection to the definition of system-level consciousness only if one first establishes `C := E`.

## “So you just renamed life consciousness.”
SCTC makes an explicit revisionary assignment. It does not hide that fact. The proposal has to earn the reassignment by comparative scientific utility and can fail against `C := E` or another rival.

## “Does this mean bacteria feel pain?”
No. `C=1` does not entail `E=1`, pain, sentience, interests, personhood, or moral status. Those are independent empirical or normative claims.

## “Does anesthesia destroy consciousness?”
Under SCTC, ordinary reversible anesthesia changes the faculty/state vector while the biological individual continues. Responsiveness can be absent and experience can be present, absent, or unknown.

## “What about brain death?”
SCTC inherits the best operational biological-individuality judgment. If one integrated viable biological individual persists, `C=1`; if only machine-maintained parts remain, `C=0`; if biological individuality is indeterminate, SCTC is indeterminate. The paper is not medical or legal guidance.

## “Why not define consciousness as experience?”
That is the named rival `C := E`. The paper requires a direct comparison rather than treating that rival definition as neutral vocabulary.

## “What about UAL or another transition marker?”
Those are capacity-transition rivals. SCTC allows that they may be important markers for sentience or phenomenal experience while denying that such a capacity is automatically the persistence condition of the biological individual.

## “Is Faculty Exclusion evidence for SCTC?”
No. It is an analytic consequence of SCTC’s primitive definition. The paper states this explicitly.

## “Can SCTC be falsified?”
Yes. The repository’s `EXPERIMENTS.md` specifies a direct nearest-rival experiment and a closed-inventory search for an independent system-level variable.
